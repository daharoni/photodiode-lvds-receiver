# Rev A schematic specifications, sheet by sheet

**Frozen 2026-10-09 (layout started): the generators are disabled (`hardware/pcb/receiver-module/gen/FROZEN`); this spec is now updated to describe GUI edits rather than drive regeneration.**

Status labels: VERIFIED (cited datasheet or measurement), PROPOSED (design intent), UNRESOLVED (open). Nothing on this page is measured or simulated. These specs are the brief for populating `hardware/pcb/receiver-module/*.kicad_sch`; the as-built notes live in `docs/schematic/<sheet>.md`. Authority: `docs/decision-log.md` (14 standing corrections), `docs/calculations/apd-bias-design.md`, `docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md`.

Conventions for every sheet:
- Symbols only from the lab library (`${AHARONI_LAB_KICAD_LIB}`); generic R/C/L get an explicit footprint. Default passives 0603 (`R_0603_1608Metric_N`, `C_0603_1608Metric_N`); 0402 only for local decoupling at fast ICs; HV parts 1206/1210 as noted.
- GND is a global label on every sheet. Rails arrive as hierarchical labels (VBUS on Power, 3V3, 3V3_D, APD_BIAS).
- Routing targets (PROPOSED, Daniel 2026-10-09): 90 ohm differential for the four SuperSpeed pairs between U50/J50 and for `CAN_H`/`CAN_L` to J50 (they ride on 90 ohm USB-C cable pairs; TC R2.5 cable pair impedance), 90 ohm differential for `USB_DP`/`USB_DM` between J50 and U61/R193/R194; intra-pair length match within 1 mm (UI 20 ns); 100 ohm differential for `LVDS_P`/`LVDS_N` from U20 to the R180 termination at U50 (short run). Trace geometry follows the stackup, which is not chosen yet (UNRESOLVED).
- Nets at or above 50 V are prefixed `HV_` (plus `APD_BIAS`); the project netclass `HV` (set in the .kicad_pro by the orchestrator) uses a 1.0 mm clearance.
- Every sheet carries a text block "Design intent" with the status labels, and every value that comes from a datasheet or the calculation docs cites it in the as-built note.
- Reference designator ranges (so sheets never collide): Power U1-U9/R1-R49 (R35-R39 used by U5, see 1.1; R40-R44 free)/C1-C49 (C32-C33 used by U5)/D1-D9/L1-L9/Q1-Q9/J1-J2/RV1-RV2/TH1/FB1-FB2/TP1-TP9; Front end U10-U19/R50-R79/C50-C79/D10-D19/JP10-JP19/TP10-TP19; Slicer U20-U29/R80-R99/C80-C99/TP20-TP29; Monitor U30-U39/R100-R149/C100-C129/D30-D39/JP30-JP39/RV30-RV39/TP30-TP39; Connector J50-J59/H1-H9 (mounting holes, reserved for this sheet; H1-H4 used)/R170-R189/C180-C189/U50-U59/TP50-TP59 (revision 4 extends R180-R189 down to R170-R179 for the shield and J51 termination; U50 widens to U50-U59 for the two ESD arrays; R150-R169 stay unallocated); Controller U60-U69/R190-R229 (R200-R202, R215, R216 freed by revision 4)/C190-C219 (C193, C205 freed; C206-C207 stay the DNP D+/D- caps)/D60-D69/J61/SW60-SW61 (RESET, BOOT)/TP60-TP69. The Slicer's R56-R59/C53-C55/R87/C87 style parts belong to the Front End/Slicer ranges above and are not touched by the Connector sheet.

Hierarchical nets (revision 4): rails `3V3`, `3V3_D`, `APD_BIAS` (Power -> users); `VBUS` (Connector -> Power and Controller; the single 5 V input, decision 14); `DAC_HV`, `HV_DIS` (Controller -> Power); `HV_MON` (Power -> Controller, Connector); `TIA_CTRL` (Controller -> Front End); `TIA_TAP` (Front End -> Monitor, RSSI pick-off); `SDET_P` (Front End -> Slicer; `SDET_N` is built on the Slicer sheet and is not hierarchical); `VCM_REF` (Monitor -> Front End, Slicer); `RSSI` (Monitor -> Controller, Connector); `DAC_TH` (Controller -> Slicer); `VTH_MON` (Slicer -> Controller); `SHDN_N`, `LE_HYS` (Controller -> Slicer); `LVDS_P/N` (Slicer -> Connector; now the input of the fan-out buffer U50); `CAN_H`, `CAN_L` (Controller <-> Connector); `AUX` (Connector <-> Controller, one net: SBU1 and SBU2 tied); `USB_DP`, `USB_DM` (Connector <-> Controller, bidirectional); `CC1_SENSE`, `CC2_SENSE` (Connector -> Controller, divided CC levels to ADC pins). Removed in revision 4: `+5V_CABLE`, `PWR_SRC`, `AUX2`, the Controller -> Power `VBUS`. `+5V` is the internal rail (U5 OUT) inside the Power sheet and is not a hierarchical net. Root: PWR_FLAG `#FLG04` moves from the `+5V_CABLE` net to the `VBUS` net; `#FLG60` (inside the Controller sheet) is removed so there is exactly one flag on VBUS.

## 1. Power sheet (`power.kicad_sch`)

Pins: in VBUS, DAC_HV, HV_DIS; out 3V3, 3V3_D, APD_BIAS, HV_MON. (Revision 2: the LM27762 +/-3V7 block, the 5V_A bead/caps, PGOOD_VGA and TP1 are removed. Revision 4: `+5V_CABLE` and `PWR_SRC` are removed.)

### 1.1 Input: eFuse U5 (TPS259631DDAR)
- U5 `AharoniLab_Power:TPS259631DDAR`, footprint `AharoniLab_Package_SO:Texas_DDA0008B_SO-PowerPAD-8` (the exact footprint name is TO BE CONFIRMED by the lab-library PR; candidate geometry from bundled `Package_SO:HSOP-8-1EP_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm_EP2.41x3.1mm` or `Package_SO:TI_SO-PowerPAD-8`, compare against the DDA land pattern, TPS2596 SLVSET8A p.46: UNRESOLVED until the PR). New lab-library symbol and footprint required. Variant chosen for x1 auto-retry (TPS2596 Device Comparison p.3); fixed-clamp variants rejected (a sustained 12 V input would dissipate about 1.6 W in the FET, arithmetic). Pin map (TPS2596 p.4, VERIFIED per the draft): 1 GND, 2 dVdt, 3 EN/UVLO, 4 IN, 5 OUT, 6 FLT, 7 ILM, 8 OVLO, exposed pad GND. Abs max on IN 21 V (TPS2596 p.1, p.5).
- IN (pin 4) = `VBUS` directly (hierarchical input, source on the Connector sheet). OUT (pin 5) = `+5V`, the internal rail used by U1, U3 (via the L2 pi filter) and U4; C1 10 uF 0805 and C2 100 nF stay on `+5V`.
- Divider (all 1 %, 0603): R35 357 k from IN to EN/UVLO, R36 43.2 k from EN/UVLO to OVLO, R37 100 k from OVLO to GND. Thresholds (PROPOSED, calculated with datasheet tolerances and 1 % resistors, not simulated; draft section 3d and `docs/research/usbc-power-path.md` item 1): overvoltage cut-off 5.76-6.20 V rising (below the 7.0 V abs max of the TPS79901, TPS799 SBVS056L p.4); UVLO 4.06-4.32 V rising, 3.72-4.00 V falling.
- ILM (pin 7): R38 1.21 k 1 % to GND, never open: ILIM 0.68 / 0.76 / 0.84 A min/typ/max (TPS2596 Eq. 5 p.22, +-10.4 %; PROPOSED arithmetic). The ILM node is also a test point TP3 "IMON" (about 0.79 V/A, usbc-power-path.md 1.4). The 0.76 A limit is a real current limit, not a USB budget: a laptop port still decides the USB 0.5 A budget.
- dVdt (pin 2): C32 47 nF X7R 10 V 0603 to GND: about 0.94 V/ms, 5.3 ms ramp, about 23 mA inrush into the assumed 25 uF on +5V (25 uF is an estimate, PROPOSED, measure in G1).
- VBUS input capacitor C33 4.7 uF 25 V X5R 0805, as close to IN as possible. C34 removed.
- FLT (pin 6): open-drain, test point TP4 "FLT"; optional R39 100 k pull-up, DNP (draft: to +5V; the brief said 3V3_D). UNRESOLVED: both rails are off while the eFuse is cut off, so a pull-up to either reads 0 V and cannot separate fault from no power; a pull-up to VBUS would read correctly. Fit only for bench use, pick the rail at generator time and check the FLT pin abs max (not read). FLT is NOT routed to the ESP32 (unpowered during cut-off). VBUS-present is implied by the MCU running; no VBUS monitor in Rev A (the ADS1115 AIN3 stays the ADC zero check).
- Margin (PROPOSED arithmetic, draft 3d): a Type-C source is 4.75-5.5 V; a compliant 3 A cable drops at most 75 mV loop at 0.3 A (TC R2.5 4.4.1 p.151), so the module sees at least about 4.66 V against UV rising 4.06-4.32 V. A non-compliant 28 AWG 3 m cable would drop about 0.38 V at 0.3 A (computed), leaving about 4.37 V against the 4.32 V maximum rising threshold: UNRESOLVED, the reason for the cable rule (decision 14: <= 2 m certified passive full-featured; 3 m only after a bench check).
- Removed: TPS2121 U5, old R35/R36 priority divider, old R37 ST pull-up, old R39-R42 OV dividers, C34, nets `+5V_CABLE` and `PWR_SRC`, PWR_FLAG on `+5V_CABLE` (the flag moves to `VBUS` at the root). TPS2121 settling/inrush G1 items are replaced (gates.md).
- Budget: worst case about 0.19 A (0.24 A with 25 % margin) from `+5V`, against the 0.5 A USB default (docs/research/usb-standalone-power.md section 1.5; datasheet maxima plus an assumed 70 % LT8365 efficiency, not measured), plus up to 35 mA on 3V3 for U50 (LVDS104 SLLS396G p.5, all four outputs loaded). Radio must stay disabled. UNRESOLVED: whether a bus-powered hub holds a module to 100 mA; document "use a laptop root port or a powered hub".
- Fallback only if Daniel withdraws the new-part approval (not planned): TPS2121 with IN1 = VBUS and IN2, OV2, CP2, PR1, ST tied to GND (draft section 3d).

### 1.2 3V3: TPS79901DDCT (U1)
- IN = +5V with 1 uF 0603 at the pin; EN tied to IN; GND.
- FB divider: VOUT = VFB x (1 + R1/R2), VFB 1.193 V typ (TPS799 SBVS056L). R1 17.8 k, R2 10.0 k, 1 %, 0603 -> 3.316 V. CFB 1 nF across R1 (value UNRESOLVED, see power.md). OUT = 3V3: 2 x 4.7 uF 0805 X7R 10 V.
- Load estimate: OPA857 26.8 mA max + OPA855 (SBOS622D, check Iq at 3.3 V) + TLV3605 17.5 mA max + OPA2320 ~2 mA ~ 65 mA. PROPOSED.

### 1.5 3V3_D digital rail: TPS7A2033PDBV (U4)
- As built: IN = +5V with 1 uF; OUT = 3V3_D with 10 uF 0805 + 1 uF; EN tied to IN. Radio must stay disabled (300 mA LDO). PROPOSED.

### 1.4 APD bias: LT8365EMSE (U3)
- As built per `docs/calculations/apd-bias-design.md` sections 3.2 and 4, with the HV disable gate (Q3 on EN/UVLO, gate pulled up to 3V3_D, driven by HV_DIS, J1 "HV FORCE ON" DNP jumper) and the HV_MON divider. Unchanged in revision 2; input from +5V (U5 OUT, the eFuse output in revision 4). Revision 3: USB-alone, HV_DIS default-off and the DAC_HV safe-end rule are unchanged; firmware starts HV at the low-bias end of DAC_HV in steps, watching HV_MON and supply sag (a faulted HV start can trip a USB port; PROPOSED). The LT8365 EN/UVLO (R22/R23, rising 3.15-3.80 V) does not guard against USB sag in the legal range.

## 3. Slicer (`slicer.kicad_sch`)

Pins: in SDET_P, VCM_REF, DAC_TH, 3V3, SHDN_N, LE_HYS; out LVDS_P, LVDS_N, VTH_MON.
- U20 TLV3605RVKR (SNOSDA2F): IN+ (4) = SDET_P (arrives already coupled and returned to VCM_REF on the Front End sheet); IN- (6) = `SDET_N` = threshold node: R84 1 k from VCM_REF to SDET_N (matches the IN+ return, bias-current cancellation), DAC_TH injected through R85 100 k into SDET_N (authority about +/-16 mV around VCM_REF when the DAC spans 0-3.3 V with VCM_REF at 1.65 V: trim = (DAC - 1.65) x 1k/101k; state the number), C84 10 nF from SDET_N to GND (keeps IN- quiet). VTH_MON = SDET_N through R86 10 k (hierarchical output to the ADC).
- External hysteresis 2-4 mV (research doc section 4): R87 from OUT+ (LVDS, ~350 mV swing into the 100 ohm termination R180 at the fan-out buffer U50 on the Connector sheet, a few cm away; revision 4) to IN+ with the IN+ source impedance being the 1 k return in parallel with the coupling path; compute R87 for ~3 mV (about 100 k with a 1 k source; show the arithmetic on the sheet and label PROPOSED; footprint 0603). LE/HYS pin: R82 to GND footprint DNP (internal hysteresis off = floating; the smallest internal step ~8 mV is too large), R83 0 ohm DNP to LE_HYS for the latch option, as before.
- VCCI (2) = 3V3 with 100 nF 0402 + 1 uF; VCCO (1) = 3V3 via R80 0 ohm with 100 nF 0402 + 1 uF; VEE and EP = GND; SHDN (7) = SHDN_N with R81 10 k pull-up to 3V3; OUT+ (12) = LVDS_P, OUT- (10) = LVDS_N.
- Revision 4 note: `LVDS_P`/`LVDS_N` now go to the Connector sheet's fan-out input U50 (A/B). The 100 ohm termination (R180, 0.1 %) sits across U50 A/B (the buffer has no on-chip termination, LVDS104 p.16 Fig. 25), so the old DNP 100 ohm bench pad on the Connector sheet is absorbed into the fitted R180. The load is still 100 ohm, so the R87/C87 hysteresis tap and the 3.0 mV window are unaffected. Layout: short 100 ohm differential route to U50. No schematic change on this sheet except the text notes (termination moved). PROPOSED.
- TP20 SDET_N, TP21 SDET_P (DNP).
- Text: baseline slicing rationale (8b/10b DC balance), bias current 1-5 uA x 1 k = 1-5 mV offset (trimmed by DAC_TH), hysteresis arithmetic, SHDN not a guaranteed zero (standing correction 11).
