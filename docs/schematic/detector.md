## Datasheet facts used

- VERIFIED: OPA356 input common-mode range (V-) - 0.1 V to (V+) - 1.5 V (DS356 p.3). With 3V3 the upper limit is 1.8 V, with 5V_A it is 3.5 V, so 5V_A is required for an SDET swing about VCM_DET (1.65 V).
- VERIFIED: supply specified 2.7 V to 5.5 V, operating 2.5 V to 5.5 V (DS356 p.3, p.10). GBW 200 MHz (G = +10), slew 300/-360 V/us (p.3). 5V_A is within range.
- VERIFIED, changed a value: "A 10 uF ceramic bypass capacitor is the minimum recommended value", located at the V+ pin (DS356 p.10). The spec asked for 100 nF + 1 uF; a 10 uF 0805 was added at each OPA356 (C154, C157). PROPOSED that the 100 nF and 1 uF stay for HF decoupling.
- VERIFIED: OPA2320 supply 1.8 V to 5.5 V, rail-to-rail input with VCM (V-) - 0.1 V to (V+) + 0.1 V, GBW 20 MHz, slew 10 V/us, input bias 0.9 pA max class (DS320 p.1, p.7-8; 3V3 is in range).
- UNRESOLVED: BAS70 forward drop (about 0.3 V, spec figure) is not checked here against a cited BAS70 datasheet; the drop depends on charging current and temperature.
- UNRESOLVED: droop tau = 1 M x 47 pF = 47 us (arithmetic only). OPA356 stability with the diode and 47 pF load, and the SDET common-mode level at the buffers, are untested.

## ERC (Detector sheet)

One remaining item: `power_pin_not_driven` on U40 V+ (5V_A). The Power sheet does not yet drive 5V_A with a power output or PWR_FLAG at the time of this check; it clears when that net is driven. Previously fixed: DET_IN bus now wired to both U40 and U41 + pins.

## G2 tests and alternatives

G2 compares VHIGH/VLOW against eye-histogram high/low levels over pattern density, bias and temperature; measure droop, diode drop and jitter added to SDET (JP40 closed vs open). Alternatives: two-diode drop-cancelling or in-loop detector, faster precision rectifier, ADC capture in the MCU (optional only).
