## Notes
- No SAMD51 on Rev A; SAMD51 DACs are 12-bit; ADC inputs to be buffered later (UNRESOLVED).
- DAC_HV 0 V vs Hi-Z (docs/calculations/apd-bias-design.md s.3.2 "Range", hand calculation, PROPOSED): DAC span +/-30.6 V (LSB 14.9 mV); both DAC and pot at 0 V = 215 V maximum; both at 3.3 V = 55 V; DAC Hi-Z at reset = 135.9 V. With R192 fitted and no DAC, DAC_HV is 0 V = maximum trim, not 135.9 V.
- Pull-down polarity: UNRESOLVED; may become pull-ups once DAC polarity is decided.
