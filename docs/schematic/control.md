## What the sheet is for

- VCM_REF (1.65 V) is the common baseline of every AC-coupled stage after the OPA857: post-amp input return and OPA855 gain reference (Front End), comparator input returns (Slicer). With 8b/10b the eye centre is the AC-coupled baseline, so no tracking threshold is needed. PROPOSED.
- RSSI is a slow, relative received-light indicator (peak detector with a diode drop behind a 1 k tap resistor on the Front End, tau 1 ms). It is telemetry for the ESP32 (ADS1115) to trim APD bias and the OPA857 gain, not a calibrated measurement. PROPOSED.
- Removed (decision 12): VMID, VSPAN, VCM_DET, VTARGET, VG_HI/VG_LO, VLOS_TH references, AGC integrator, TMUX1112 freeze, TLV3201 LOS comparator, VG/VTH selectors, the old U31/U32/U33 and their trimmers/clamps. The VCA821 and analog AGC no longer exist.

## Datasheet facts and reasoning

- VERIFIED: the OPA857 photodiode cathode goes to a positive bias and the anode to IN; the photodiode then sources a current that makes OUT swing DOWN below the reference (5/9 x 3.3 V = 1.83 V), and OUT can only swing down from the reference (TIA 7.3.1 PDF p.15; 8.1 PDF p.17; 8.2.1.2 step 2 PDF p.18). So the signal peak is a negative excursion and D30 has its cathode on TIA_TAP and anode on RSSI_HOLD; RSSI falls as light rises.
- VERIFIED: OPA320 unity-gain buffer is stable with a pure capacitive load up to approximately 1 nF; a 10 to 20 ohm series resistor is the technique for larger loads; ESR of capacitors above 1 uF can help (OPA 7.3.8, PDF p.19, Figures 36 and 37). Supply 1.8 V to 5.5 V (OPA p.1). The 1 uF load with R102 = 10 ohm is beyond the datasheet's illustrated cases. SIMULATED (ngspice 47, TI OPAx320 Final 1.6, one channel of OPA2320; ideal 3.3 V supply, three 1 k resistive loads, no capacitive loads downstream, second channel not included): R102 = 10 ohm gives 87.6 deg phase margin, 2.05 MHz loop crossover, 0.25 dB sensitivity peak, 0 % overshoot at VCM_REF on a 10 mV step and settling to 0.05 mV in 28 us; a 100 mV disturbance through 1 k moves VCM_REF by 0.96 mV (about 1 %), first order (tau about R102 x C101 = 10 us). The control case with R102 = 0.01 ohm is unstable in the model (1.2 deg phase margin, 33.3 dB peaking and ringing near 190 kHz), consistent with the datasheet's roughly 1 nF direct-load limit. So 10 ohm is sufficient in the model and the value stands; 22 and 47 ohm also pass (86.2 and 84.1 deg) but only cost DC accuracy. Stability on the real board stays UNRESOLVED until the VCM_REF step is measured (G1); the simulated load case draws 1.65 mA per 1 k load, whereas real return currents are microamps.
- PROPOSED: VCM_REF is taken after R102, with the amplifier feedback from the op-amp pin. The loads are a few 1 k returns, so the DC drop across 10 ohm is negligible.
- PROPOSED: tau = 1 M x 1 nF = 1 ms. R103 returns RSSI_HOLD to 3V3 (not VCM_REF): at idle about (3.3 - 1.83) V / 1 M = 1.5 uA flows through D30 and the 1 k tap into the TIA output (about 1.5 mV, negligible). RSSI_HOLD idles near OUTN + Vf (roughly 1.9 to 2.0 V; Vf at 1.5 uA not looked up) and falls as light rises.
- PROPOSED: this removes the dead zone a VCM_REF return would have had (D30 off until the TIA fell 0.3 to 0.5 V below idle, since TIA_TAP idles at 1.83 V against 1.65 V). Idle level depends on Vf, so RSSI stays a relative indicator. Not simulated or measured.
