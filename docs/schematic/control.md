## What the loop does, in plain words

- The slicer's peak detectors give VHIGH (top of the eye) and VLOW (bottom). VSPAN = VHIGH - VLOW is the eye height. VMID = (VHIGH + VLOW) / 2 is the eye midpoint.
- The AGC integrator compares VSPAN with VTARGET (0.994 V) and moves VINT slowly (tau 4.7 ms) until they are equal. VINT drives VG, the VCA821 gain control.
- Sign: VSPAN enters the inverting input, so VSPAN above VTARGET makes VINT ramp down, VG down, gain down (VCA gain rises with VG over 0 to 2 V, VCA p1), eye smaller. This is negative feedback and the integrator removes the steady-state error.
- Freeze: two TMUX1112 switches in series in the VSPAN -> R104 path. Switch 1 is always closed (SEL1 tied to 3V3 through fitted 0R R120; LOS_N reaches SEL1 only through DNP 0R R121, bench option). If HOLD_N is pulled low by the FPGA, switch 2 opens, R104 carries no current, C102 keeps its charge and VINT holds. It is a hold, not a reset: a reset would discard the last good gain and force re-acquisition from a fixed start after every fade.
- Design decision (orchestrator): the analog LOS comparator does NOT freeze the integrator (startup deadlock: low eye -> LOS_N low -> frozen -> gain can never rise). With no signal the loop drives VG toward its upper bound (SEARCH state at maximum gain, docs/architecture.md "States" table). The FPGA's HOLD_N is the only freeze. LOS_N remains the LOS status output.
- JP30 selects the VG source (VINT, manual trimmer, or DAC through R116). JP31 selects the VTH source (VMID, trimmer, DAC through R118).

## Datasheet facts used

- VERIFIED: TMUX1112 switch is ON with SELx logic 1 and OFF with logic 0 (TMUX Table 8-2, p24; also the description on p1: "Logic 1 is required to turn on switches in the TMUX1112"; the device comparison table calls it normally open, p2). VIH is 1.35 V max at VDD = 3.3 V (p8), so a 3.3 V push-pull TLV3201 output and an FPGA 3.3 V output both drive SELx. Supply 1.08 V to 5.5 V (p1); VDD decoupling 0.1 uF to 10 uF (pin table, p3). Unused logic pins to GND or VDD and unused Sx/Dx to GND (p24, section 9 text); applied to S3/S4/SEL3/SEL4, drains left NC.
- VERIFIED: series pair means both SEL1 and SEL2 must be 1 for the loop to run (SEL1 is held at 1 by R120). On-resistance 2 ohm typ (p1) is negligible against 100k. Leakage is pA-level (p1, 3 pA typ) so droop of C102 through the open switch is small, not quantified here.
- VERIFIED: OPA2320 supply 1.8 V to 5.5 V (OPA p6); true rail-to-rail input, VCM (V-) - 0.1 V to (V+) + 0.1 V (OPA p7, p16); output swing within 25 mV typ of both rails at 2 kohm (OPA p8); input bias 0.2 pA typ (OPA p7); local bypass 0.001 uF to 0.1 uF (OPA p16). 3V3 supply and 100 nF each are within these. Rail-to-rail input means the 0.99 V IN+ and the VHIGH/2 node need no level shift.
- VERIFIED: TLV3201 supply 2.7 V to 5.5 V, push-pull output, 1.2 mV internal hysteresis, input range (VEE) - 0.2 V to (VCC) + 0.2 V (TLV p1, p5). External non-inverting hysteresis uses a series resistor R1 on the input and R2 from OUT to IN+ with VHYST = VCC x R1 / R2 (TLV p14, Figure 8-5, Eq. 6). R114 = 10k, R113 = 1M gives 3.3 x 10k / 1M = 33 mV, so trips at about VSPAN 0.303 V rising and 0.270 V falling for VLOS_TH = 0.30 V. Calculated, not simulated; output swing offset ignored.
- VERIFIED: VCA821 gain is linear in dB over VG = 0 V to +2 V; rising VG raises gain (VCA p1). That fixes the loop sign. The datasheet condition is +/-5 V supplies; behaviour at +/-3.7 V is UNRESOLVED.
- Calculated values (PROPOSED): VTARGET = 3.3 x 10.0 / 33.2 = 0.994 V; VG_HI = 3.3 x 20 / 33 = 2.0 V; VG_LO = 3.3 x 6.8 / 106.8 = 0.21 V; VLOS_TH = 3.3 x 10 / 110 = 0.30 V; tau = 100k x 47 nF = 4.7 ms (decision log 9).

## Changes from the spec and datasheet-driven choices

- C102 is placed as 47 nF C0G in a 1206 footprint (`C_1206_3216Metric_N`): 47 nF C0G is not generally stocked in 0603. PROPOSED; a film capacitor is the alternative. Stock not checked.
- The freeze switch pair is S1/S2 chained on the left of U33 with D1 as input and D2 as output (both on the right pins). Functionally identical to the spec.
- Unused switches: spec said inputs tied low and outputs NC. Implemented as Sx and SELx to GND with Dx NC, per the TMUX p24 guidance.
- VG and VTH selectors: pin 2 (common) is the output, so with no jumper fitted VG/VTH float unless R116/R118 is fitted. Stated on the sheet.
- Test points: the spec lists LOS_N, VINT, VSPAN, VMID, VTARGET, VG, VTH; all seven placed (TP30-TP36), none DNP.
- The LOS hierarchical label is named `LOS` and sits on net `LOS_N`; ERC reports `multiple_net_names` (warning, expected, spec asks for both names).
- Hierarchical labels: inputs 3V3, VHIGH, VLOW, DAC_GAIN, DAC_TH, HOLD_N; outputs VCM_DET, VG, VTH, LOS, VMID, VSPAN.
- Power units of each OPA2320 are drawn as separate unit-3 symbols with their own 100 nF; rails are connected by labels (3V3 local label joins the 3V3 hierarchical label; GND is a global label).

## UNRESOLVED

- VG upper bound: with no signal the integrator winds toward its upper bound; the bound versus the VCA821 2 V gain-control range is UNRESOLVED (D30 clamp DNP).
- Anti-windup acting on the state is only partly designed: freeze covers HOLD only. D30/D31 clamp VINT (the op-amp output) to a soft band and do not stop the integrator capacitor charging while the output is clamped. Behaviour at the rails is open. D30/D31 are DNP.
- Loop gain, phase margin, the VCA821 gain law at +/-3.7 V, and recovery after a fade are neither simulated nor measured (G3).
- The peak detectors are loaded by R102/R103 and R43/R44 (about 20k across VHIGH/VLOW each); slicer droop not checked.
- C102 charge injection from the TMUX switches and leakage are not quantified; C102 part availability not checked.
- VLOS_TH 0.30 V and the 33 mV hysteresis are first guesses; span alone does not prove signal presence.
- The VMID buffer with 100 pF input capacitor and the VG output cable load on OPA320 (capacitive load drive, OPA p8 "see Typical Characteristics") are not checked.
