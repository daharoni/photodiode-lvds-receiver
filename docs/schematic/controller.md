# Controller sheet (`controller.kicad_sch`, as built, revision 4 / decisions 12, 13 and 14)

Generator: `hardware/pcb/receiver-module/gen/controller.py`. Spec: `docs/schematic/rev-a-sheet-specs.md` section 7.

## Role (PROPOSED)
Slow trims and housekeeping only, never the data path: range trim (DAC_HV toward the Q(M) optimum; TIA_CTRL 20k/5k scheduling from RSSI and FPGA validity), threshold trim (DAC_TH, read back on VTH_MON), HV gating (HV_DIS; HV_MON readback), module identity and status over CAN. Hierarchical pins: in 3V3_D, HV_MON, RSSI, VTH_MON, VBUS, CC1_SENSE, CC2_SENSE; out DAC_TH, DAC_HV, HV_DIS, SHDN_N, LE_HYS, TIA_CTRL; bidirectional USB_DP, USB_DN, CAN_P (CANH/CANL; renamed from CAN_H/CAN_L 2026-10-16 so the KiCad diff-pair router pairs them), CAN_N, AUX (revision 4: PWR_SRC in, VBUS out and AUX2 removed; VBUS is now an input; USB_DP/USB_DN are drawn bidirectional as on the Connector sheet).

Unprogrammed ESP32: the module decodes. TIA_CTRL floats and the Front End pull-up gives 20 k; SHDN_N pull-up keeps the comparator on. HV_DIS is pulled up on the Power sheet, so an unprogrammed module has NO APD bias; bench jumper J1 (HV FORCE ON, Power sheet) forces it on. Facts and citations: `docs/research/module-controller.md` (section numbers below). Status labels: VERIFIED (datasheet cited), PROPOSED, UNRESOLVED. Nothing here is measured or simulated; ERC and netlist export only (kicad-cli 10.0.5, scratch copy of the project with the root pointing at this sheet).

IO15 and IO21 are no_connect. IO4/IO5 are strapping pins (MTMS/MTDI) that only select SDIO sampling edges (MINI-1 datasheet Table 4-4 p.14); SDIO unused, PROPOSED harmless.

Firmware states (PROPOSED; `firmware/esp32/README.md`): `cc_class` and `cc_orient` are logged hints, `usb_host` = USB Serial/JTAG SOF seen (TRM v1.2 section 32.5 p.1035); the definitive system indicator is LINK_ALL from the FPGA master. CAN TX stays silent until bus traffic or the FPGA master is seen. The ESP32 must not clear HV_DIS on the basis of CC levels alone. UNRESOLVED: firmware policy for the D+ pull-up backdrive into an unpowered host (TRM p.1042); the self-test cannot prove the comparator or LVDS output without an FPGA or optical source; ground offset between laptop and FPGA supply not analysed; the eFuse limit (about 0.76 A) can reset the module, so the boot log records the reset reason.

## Deviations from the spec
- IO10 and IO11 do not exist on the MINI-1 (MINI datasheet v1.5 Table 3-1, p10-11: 22 GPIOs). LDAC moved to IO6 (pad 15) and AUX to IO7 (pad 16). These are the JTAG MTCK/MTDO pins; USB-Serial-JTAG is the debug path, so external JTAG is given up. PROPOSED.
- ESP32-C6 (U60), USBLC6 (U61) and SN65HVD230 (U64) power pins sit on the opposite side of the symbol from what a first reading suggests (3V3/VBUS/VCC on top, GND at the bottom); wiring follows the library symbols.
- IO15 is a strapping pin, so TIA_CTRL uses IO20 (the old LOS input). The TP numbering stays within TP60-TP69; the status LED has no test pad.
- The ADS1115 ALERT/RDY pin is a no_connect (spec item: IO23 is the LED).
- DAC rows use local nets DACO_A..D before the RC filter; the filtered OUTA/OUTD nets are `DAC_A` (TP68) and `DAC_D` (TP69).
