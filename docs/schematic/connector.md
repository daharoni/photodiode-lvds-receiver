U51/U52 pins 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 are NC pins in TI SLVSBO7O rev O (Aug 2024) Table 4-3 p.5: "Not connected; used for optional straight-through routing. Can be left floating or grounded." They are not internally connected to the I/O pins (VERIFIED, datasheet). Straight-through routing means the protected trace enters the I/O pad and leaves under the package body through the opposite NC pad (TI Fig. 7-11 p.17 layout example, drawn for the TPD4E05U06). Since 2026-10-16 the lab-library symbol (AharoniLab_Misc:TPD6E05U06, lab library branch tpd6e05u06-straight-through-pins, PR pending) draws the six NC pins as hidden passive pins stacked on the I/O pins (1 on 14, 2 on 13, 3 on 12, 4 on 11, 6 on 9, 7 on 8; KiCad library convention KLC S4.3), so each NC pad carries the same net as its opposite pad. Netlist (VERIFIED): U52 pads 1/2/3/4/6/7 = LVDS_OUT1_P / LVDS_OUT1_N / CAN_P / CAN_N / CC2 / AUX_CAB; U51 pads 1/3/4/6/7 = CC1 / LVDS_OUT2_P / LVDS_OUT2_N / CAN_P / CAN_N (6/7 swapped 2026-10-22; were CAN_N / CAN_P); U51 pad 2 shares the unconnected net of the spare pin 13 (the no_connect flag sits on the stacked pair; ERC 0/0). Layout rule (PROPOSED): route each protected line connector pad -> clamp I/O pad -> under the body -> opposite pad -> onward; this is what the 2026-10-09 "straight top-layer traces in pad order" note intended. The straight-through pads add 12 pad connections to the DRC unconnected list until routed.

ESD channel split by position (hand edit 2026-10-09, after placement started; generators frozen): the revision-4 split (U51 = both TX pairs + both CC, U52 = both CAN pairs + SBU) made half the protected lines run the length of the J50 pad rows past the other device before reaching their clamp, because the two plug orientations put the same function at opposite ends of the receptacle. U51 now clamps the lower-end pins (A5, A3/A2, B10/B11) and U52 the upper-end pins (B2/B3, A11/A10, B5, A8/B8), each pin level with its pad: A-row SMD pads get straight top-layer traces in pad order, B-row through-hole pins arrive on an inner layer with a via at the clamp pad, and pair polarity follows the pad order (ESD channels are identical). Nets unchanged; ERC 0/0; 223 components. PROPOSED.

Fan-out output swap (hand edit 2026-10-09): U50 output 1 (pins 16/15, the upper pair on the TSSOP) now feeds the upper end of J50 (B2/B3 through U52) and output 2 (pins 14/13) the lower end (A2/A3 through U51), so the two pairs no longer cross between U50 and the clamps. Polarity flip at J50 (Daniel, same day): N to SSTXp and P to SSTXn on both pairs. The lower pair now matches the package order (2Y above 2Z onto A3 above A2) with no crossing from U50 through U51 to the pads, all on the top layer. The upper pair reaches U52 uncrossed (1Y on pin 14, 1Z on pin 13) and swaps between the clamp pins and the B2/B3 barrels, where the lines already leave the top layer to get past the A-row pads. The polarity is consistent on both pairs, so plug orientation still does not matter; the cable carries the fan-out output inverted, and with LVDS_P low for a logic one (frontend.md) SSTXp is high for a one. The FPGA-end inversion constant moves accordingly (gateware, breakout docs). PROPOSED.

- Fan-out margin: TLV3605 VOD 250/350/450 mV (TLV p.7) against the buffer threshold +-100 mV: 150 mV at minimum VOD (VERIFIED arithmetic). Fail-safe static high with no TLV3605 power (LVDS104 p.15-16). Supply 3V3 up to about 101 mA total (66 mA estimate + 35 mA), inside the TPS79901 200 mA rating (rating not rechecked: UNRESOLVED). The ECP5 end is still terminated on-die (DIFFRESISTOR 100, `docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md`); R180 sits at U50, not the FPGA.
- CC taps: 100k/100k keeps the pin below 3.3 V with 5.5 V VCONN on the unused CC (2.75 V, TC R2.5 p.155); a compliant source maximum 2.04 V becomes 1.02 V (TC R2.0 Table 4-36 p.241); the tap lowers effective Rd by about 2.5 %, Thevenin 50 k with 100 nF is 5 ms.
- SBU/AUX: single-ended, <= about 1 MHz (TC Table 3-24 p.113); a driven AUX line against TC section 4.3 p.150 (SBU open or weakly pulled down) is accepted; firmware holds AUX high-Z until LINK_ALL or the host class is known. UNRESOLVED: laptop alternate-mode SBU drive, SBU voltage limits.
- CAN: no termination on the module (SLLA270 p.4, p.8 Fig. 10); bus on both RX pairs gives about 45 ohm in parallel (PROPOSED). 125 kbit/s PROPOSED.
- ESD (TPD Table 5.4 p.7, VERIFIED): 0.47 pF typ, VRWM 5.5 V; place within a few mm of J50 (TPD p.5). UNRESOLVED: unidirectional clamp near -0.7 V against cable ground drop on CAN; USON-14 footprint versus the TI drawing.
- ULX3S targets gp/gn[8..13] (bank 7) then [14..21]: VERIFIED, ULX3S v3.1.7 p.2; Lattice FPGA-TN-02032 v1.4 p.14, p.26.
- HV rule: no bare APD_BIAS pad; HV_MON (about Vout/101) is the only HV observation point (PROPOSED).
- Cable rule and labelling text are on the sheet (passive full-featured USB 3.2 Gen 1, 3 A, e-marked, <= 2 m; the SuperSpeed pairs carry proprietary LVDS/CAN, not USB 3).
