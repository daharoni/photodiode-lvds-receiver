# Connector sheet (as built)

Generated by `hardware/pcb/receiver-module/gen/connector.py`. Spec: `rev-a-sheet-specs.md` section 6 (revision 2026-10-08, decision 11). Nothing here is simulated or measured. Labels: VERIFIED / PROPOSED / UNRESOLVED.

## Hierarchical pins
Outputs +5V, HOLD_N; inputs LVDS_P, LVDS_N, LOS, VMID, VSPAN, HV_MON, 3V3, +3V7, -3V7, 5V_A, 3V3_D; bidirectional CAN_H, CAN_L, AUX. SHDN_N and LE_HYS are no longer on this sheet (driven by the Controller sheet); R181 is removed.

## J50 "MODULE CABLE" pinout (Wuerth 61201221621, 2x06 shrouded, 2.54 mm; PROPOSED)
Source: `docs/research/module-controller.md` section 6 (reasoning is PROPOSED, not measured). Pins 2n-1/2n share a row.

| Row | Odd pin | Even pin | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 +5V | 2 +5V | shared supply current, first row away from signals |
| 2 | 3 GND | 4 GND | power return |
| 3 | 5 LVDS_P | 6 LVDS_N | 50 Mbps pair on adjacent pins |
| 4 | 7 GND | 8 GND | ground row between LVDS and CAN |
| 5 | 9 CAN_H | 10 CAN_L | CAN pair |
| 6 | 11 HOLD_N | 12 AUX | control; AUX reserved (ESP32 GPIO10) |

The research doc lists pin 12 as SPARE (to GND at both ends if unused); the spec makes it AUX. Keying/shield on the cable: UNRESOLVED (research doc section 6). Library pin names are Pin_1..Pin_12.

## Rules and citations
- ULX3S targets gp/gn[8..13] (bank 7) then [14..21] (banks 2/3): VERIFIED, ULX3S v3.1.7 p.2; Lattice FPGA-TN-02032 v1.4 p.14, p.26 (`docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md`).
- LVDS: on-die DIFFRESISTOR 100 ohm at the FPGA, VERIFIED TN p.15, p.20, p.26. Fitting R180 as well gives ~50 ohm (arithmetic, not measured). ECP5 LVDS input common-mode range: UNRESOLVED (FPGA-DS-02012 not obtained).
- CAN (`docs/research/module-controller.md` section 3): no termination on modules; one split termination (2 x 60 ohm 1 % + 4.7 nF) at the FPGA-end junction (PROPOSED; SLLA270 p.4 advises against termination on a removable node, VERIFIED; split termination shown in SLLA270 p.8 Fig. 10, VERIFIED). R182/R183/C180 here are for a single-module bench only. Bit rate 125 kbit/s PROPOSED. FPGA-side CAN node: UNRESOLVED.
- HV rule: no bare APD_BIAS pad; HV_MON (~Vout/101) is the only HV observation point (PROPOSED).
