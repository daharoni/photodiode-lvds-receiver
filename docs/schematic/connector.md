U51 now clamps the lower-end pins (A5, A3/A2, B10/B11) and U52 the upper-end pins (B2/B3, A11/A10, B5, A8/B8), each pin level with its pad: A-row SMD pads get straight top-layer traces in pad order, B-row through-hole pins arrive on an inner layer with a via at the clamp pad, and pair polarity follows the pad order (ESD channels are identical). ## Rules and citations
- Fan-out margin: TLV3605 VOD 250/350/450 mV (TLV p.7) against the buffer threshold +-100 mV: 150 mV at minimum VOD (VERIFIED arithmetic). Fail-safe static high with no TLV3605 power (LVDS104 p.15-16). Supply 3V3 up to about 101 mA total (66 mA estimate + 35 mA), inside the TPS79901 200 mA rating (rating not rechecked: UNRESOLVED). The ECP5 end is still terminated on-die (DIFFRESISTOR 100, `docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md`); R180 sits at U50, not the FPGA.
- CC taps: 100k/100k keeps the pin below 3.3 V with 5.5 V VCONN on the unused CC (2.75 V, TC R2.5 p.155); a compliant source maximum 2.04 V becomes 1.02 V (TC R2.0 Table 4-36 p.241); the tap lowers effective Rd by about 2.5 %, Thevenin 50 k with 100 nF is 5 ms.
- SBU/AUX: single-ended, <= about 1 MHz (TC Table 3-24 p.113); a driven AUX line against TC section 4.3 p.150 (SBU open or weakly pulled down) is accepted; firmware holds AUX high-Z until LINK_ALL or the host class is known. UNRESOLVED: laptop alternate-mode SBU drive, SBU voltage limits.
- CAN: no termination on the module (SLLA270 p.4, p.8 Fig. 10); bus on both RX pairs gives about 45 ohm in parallel (PROPOSED). 125 kbit/s PROPOSED.
- ESD (TPD Table 5.4 p.7, VERIFIED): 0.47 pF typ, VRWM 5.5 V; place within a few mm of J50 (TPD p.5). UNRESOLVED: unidirectional clamp near -0.7 V against cable ground drop on CAN; USON-14 footprint versus the TI drawing.
- ULX3S targets gp/gn[8..13] (bank 7) then [14..21]: VERIFIED, ULX3S v3.1.7 p.2; Lattice FPGA-TN-02032 v1.4 p.14, p.26.
- HV rule: no bare APD_BIAS pad; HV_MON (about Vout/101) is the only HV observation point (PROPOSED).
- Cable rule and labelling text are on the sheet (passive full-featured USB 3.2 Gen 1, 3 A, e-marked, <= 2 m; the SuperSpeed pairs carry proprietary LVDS/CAN, not USB 3).
