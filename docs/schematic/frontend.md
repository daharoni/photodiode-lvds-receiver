# Front End sheet as-built (`frontend.kicad_sch`, revision 2, decision 12)

Generated by `hardware/pcb/receiver-module/gen/frontend.py` (spec: `rev-a-sheet-specs.md` section 2; basis: `docs/research/post-amp-slicer.md`). Nothing here is measured; ERC and the kicad-cli netlist only confirm connectivity. Three ngspice simulations with TI vendor models exist (`docs/simulation/README.md`, labelled SIMULATED below; not VERIFIED, no parasitics, no tolerances). Datasheets: OPA857 SBOS630D "OPA857"; OPA855 SBOS622D "OPA855" (printed page numbers). Labels: VERIFIED (datasheet cited), SIMULATED (ngspice, model named), PROPOSED, UNRESOLVED.

Hierarchical pins: in APD_BIAS, 3V3, TIA_CTRL, VCM_REF; out SDET_P, TIA_TAP. SDET_N is built on the Slicer sheet. TIA_CTRL lands on the CTRL net (R57 pull-up, JP10).

## Signal path (left to right)
1. APD: R50 10k 1206 (200 V) from APD_BIAS to HV_K; C50 10n 1210 (500 V C0G) at the cathode; D10 S12023-10 (K = HV_K, A = TIA_IN, CASE = GND); D11 FDS010 DNP on the same nets. PROPOSED. Cathode to the positive bias and anode to IN is the OPA857 reference arrangement (OPA857 p.19, 8.2.1.2 step 2).
2. OPA857 TIA (U10): IN = TIA_IN; VS+ (9, 10, 11) = 3V3 with C56, C57 100n 0402 + C59 1u 0603; GND pins and PAD = GND; Test_SD (13) GND, Test_IN (14) 3V3 (OPA857 p.4 pin table); NC (16) open; CTRL = R57 100k pull-up to 3V3 (logic 1 = 20k) with JP10 (B = 3V3, A = GND, open by default). PROPOSED.
3. Coupling: C51 100n from TIA_OUT to PA_IN, R51 1k to VCM_REF (corner 1.6 kHz, 100 us). TIA_TAP = TIA_OUT through R58 1k. TIA_REF (OUTN) goes only to TP12.
4. OPA855 (U11), non-inverting, gain 1 + R53/R54 = 1 + 301/49.9 = 7.03. IN+ = PA_IN; RF R53 between FB (pin 1) and IN- (pin 3), drawn as a loop over the top; RG R54 from IN- to PA_REF; OUT (6) drives the load. PA_REF = VCM_REF through R60 10 ohm with C53 1u to GND. VS+ (7) = 3V3 with C52 100n 0402 + C58 1u 0603; VS- (5) and EP (9) = GND; PD (8) = 3V3 directly.
5. Low-pass: R56 49.9 + C54 47p = 68 MHz (PROPOSED; research asks 60-80 MHz).
6. C55 100n to SDET_P, R59 1k to VCM_REF. TP10 PA_OUT, TP11 SDET_P, TP12 TIA_REF.

## Datasheet facts that set values
- VERIFIED OPA855 p.17 (8.3.2, Fig. 8-4): FB (pin 1) is internally connected to the amplifier output; the feedback resistor goes between FB and IN- on the same side of the package; NC pin 2 must stay floating. OUT drives the load (the FB and OUT pins are two different external nets). Fig. 8-1 (p.16): non-inverting gain (1 + RF/RG) referenced to VREF.
- VERIFIED OPA855 p.7 (Power Down): PD low = off, high = on; enable threshold 1.5 V typ / 1.8 V max, disable below 1.0 V typ; PD bias current 70 uA typ / 140 uA max. The spec suggested 10k to the enabled level; 10k x 140 uA would drop 1.4 V and fail the 1.8 V max threshold, so PD is tied straight to 3V3, as the layout figure shows (p.29, 9.4.2: "Connect PD to VS+ to enable the amplifier").
- VERIFIED OPA855 p.20 (8.4.1) and p.29: thermal pad to VS- (GND here).
- VERIFIED OPA855 p.6: input common-mode 1.3 V max (low) to 2.7 V min (high) at VS+ = 3.3 V, so VCM_REF 1.65 V is inside; overdrive recovery 3 ns; IB -12 uA typ (-18.5 to -5).
- RF/RG: no RF/RG table exists. The datasheet's gain-7 test conditions use RF 453 ohm with RG about 75 ohm (Fig. 7-3, p.15; research doc). The spec's 301/49.9 (G = 7.03) is used: PROPOSED. SIMULATED (ngspice 47, TI OPA855 Rev A macromodel): RF 301 / RG 49.9 and the datasheet's 453 / 75 behave the same at gain 7: 77 deg phase margin for both, no peaking (0.1 dB), 2.13 GHz vs 2.36 GHz closed-loop -3 dB, and the same 0.4 % overshoot on a 20 ns edge and about 1 % on a 2 ns edge. So the sheet value 301 / 49.9 stands (no worse than the datasheet test values in the model). Caveats: the model's AC loop-gain polarity is inverted relative to its transient behaviour in ngspice (cause not found), so the 77 deg figure is moderately trustworthy (the closed-loop AC magnitude matches a transient sine response to 0.02 dB; the transient overshoot numbers are the more reliable evidence); no parasitics (FB-to-IN- layout, SBOS622D p.17, p.29, not represented), no resistor/capacitor tolerances, ideal supplies and VCM_REF, source is an ideal source behind 25 ohm, the cascade OPA857 -> OPA855 was not simulated. At gain 5 (below the datasheet minimum stable gain 7) the model shows 6.1 dB peaking near 3.1 GHz, consistent with the datasheet warning but not independently checked. UNRESOLVED until measured (G1).
- VERIFIED OPA857 p.15 (7.3.1): class-A output swings from the 1.83 V common-mode level toward the negative rail only; 1.2 V swing on 3.3 V; 60 uA at 20k, 240 uA at 5k.
- **OPA857 output polarity (VERIFIED, p.19, 8.2.1.2 step 2):** with the APD cathode on positive bias and anode on IN, photocurrent into IN makes OUT swing DOWN below the reference OUTN (1.83 V). Consequences: (a) the Monitor sheet's RSSI peak detector on TIA_TAP must track the minimum (negative peaks) of the waveform, relative to a DC level that is TIA_OUT's idle (about 1.83 V before AC coupling); (b) after the non-inverting OPA855 and AC coupling, "light on" is BELOW the VCM_REF baseline on SDET_P, so LVDS_P is low for a one: the FPGA must invert or swap the pair.

## PROPOSED design values
- Output offset: IB -12 uA into R51 1k gives about +12 mV at IN+, x7.03 = +84 mV, minus IB x RF = -3.6 mV, so about +80 mV (derived): idle about 1.73 V, near the 1.75 V mid-point of the output limits 1.15 to 2.35 V (OPA855 p.7). Output is AC coupled afterwards, so only headroom is affected. SIMULATED (ngspice 47, TI OPA855 Rev A) idle at OUT is 1.578 V, not 1.73 V: the model draws its 12 uA bias current into the pins (IN+ sits 12 mV below VCM_REF) while the datasheet table gives IB = -12 uA typ without stating the sign convention (SBOS622D 6.5, p.6). The two idle levels (derived +80 mV offset vs model) differ by about 150 mV and neither is VERIFIED; matters only for headroom. UNRESOLVED until measured (G1).
- Clip level (derived): 1.15 V / (7 x 18.2k) = 9 uA at 20k; 36.5 uA at 5k (research section 3); the TIA itself saturates at 60 / 240 uA.
- Large-signal caveat (SIMULATED, ngspice 47, TI OPA855 Rev A): the model stays linear to about 80 mV input (OUT min 1.051 V) and hits its own low limit near 0.8 V, which is 0.25 to 0.35 V BELOW the datasheet VOL (1.05 V typ / 1.15 V max, SBOS622D 6.5, p.6). Beyond the limit OUT does not sit flat but enters a limit cycle of about 0.85 V pp at 1 to 2 GHz for as long as the input stays over-driven, identical at RF 301 and 453; whether the real part does this is UNRESOLVED. The model's recovery (under 1 ns after the input is back, against the datasheet 3 ns for 2x overdrive, p.6) therefore does not describe the real part, which clips at 1.05-1.15 V where about 60 mV of input actually lands; clip and recovery stay UNRESOLVED until the G1 clip test.
- R60 10 ohm isolates the 1u PA_REF decoupling cap from the Monitor sheet's VCM_REF buffer (added part; not in the spec).

## Open items
OPA857 at 6 pF source capacitance (datasheet characterises 1.5 pF, p.6): SIMULATED (ngspice 47, TI OPA857 v1.0 behavioural model, 20 ns UI, 2 ns input edges): stable in both modes, but in 20k mode the model shows 0.97 dB peaking at 54 MHz, 13.8 % overshoot on a 5-UI run and 12.6 ns to settle to 2 % (of a 20 ns UI), against 1.7 % and 6.1 ns at 1.5 pF; 5k mode at 6 pF is clean (151 MHz, no peaking, 1.7 % overshoot, 4.7 ns settle). The 20k overshoot is signal shaping, not instability, but it is pattern-dependent ISI at 50 Mb/s that the slicer sees. Design consequence: 5k is the better mode at 6 pF whenever the signal allows and 20k is the sensitivity fallback (firmware/esp32/README.md 6.3); G1 must measure the 20k eye at 6 pF. Model fidelity at 6 pF is not validated: at 1.5 pF the model gives 106 / 127 MHz (20k / 5k) against the datasheet 105 / 125 MHz (SBOS630D 6.5, p.6), which supports but does not prove its 6 pF behaviour; the model's single-ended transimpedance is 19.5k vs the datasheet 18.2k; APD series resistance, bias-dependent capacitance, noise and dark current are not modelled; AC runs used a derived copy of the model with LIMIT() removed. UNRESOLVED until measured (G1). OPA855 RF/RG stability: SIMULATED as above, UNRESOLVED until measured. Received power not measured.
