## Datasheet facts

- VERIFIED: OPA857 Test_IN (14) "connect to +VS during normal operation", Test_SD (13) "connect to GND for normal operation" (OPA p.4 pin table; p.19 procedure step 3). CTRL high = 20k gain, low = 5k (p.6 EC: 18.2k / 4.5k into 500 ohm); CTRL VIH 2 V min, VIL 0.8 V max (p.7). OUTN is a reference, VICR 1.78/1.83/1.88 V (p.6).
- VERIFIED: layout, 0.1 uF decoupling within 0.25 in of the supply pins, 2.2 to 6.8 uF on main supply pins (OPA p.24, 10.1 b; VCA p.36 item 2). The schematic puts C55/C56 on the 3V3 line about 25 mm of net from the pins; they must be placed at the pins in layout (PROPOSED).
- VERIFIED: VCA821 DGS (VSSOP-10) pins: FB 1, +VCC 2, VG 3, +VIN 4, +RG 5, -RG 6, -VIN 7, -VCC 8, VOUT 9, GND 10 (VCA p.4). RF 402 / RG 80 is the 20 dB (G = 10) column of Table 2 (VCA p.28); RG 80.6 is the nearest 1 % value (PROPOSED). The VSSOP bandwidth is lower than the SOIC (p.28).
- VERIFIED: RG current limit 2.6 mA typ, VIN(PP) max = 2 x RG x IRG (VCA p.9 and p.24). With RG 80.6: about 0.42 Vpp differential. UNRESOLVED: the 20k TIA path (up to 60 uA linear) can exceed this near 21 uA peak. RG may need to rise; this is not resolved here.
- VERIFIED: VCA821 supply 7 V to 12 V total (VCA p.36, section 11). +/-3.7 V is 7.4 V. UNRESOLVED margin (decision-log: min +/-3.5 V).
- UNRESOLVED: OPA857 stability with a 6 pF source is not verified; datasheet curves use CS = 1.5 pF (OPA p.6).

## ERC (Front End sheet, with the root sheet as the ERC target)

- power_pin_not_driven x4 (U10 VS+, U10 GND, U11 +VCC, U11 -VCC): no power output pin or PWR_FLAG drives these nets yet. 3V3, +/-3V7 are driven by the Power sheet regulators once populated (concurrent work), and GND needs a PWR_FLAG at the root. Justified, not a sheet defect.
- pin_to_pin warning x2: lib symbol types Test_IN (14) and Test_SD (13) as Unspecified, connected to a power or passive pin. Intended per the datasheet; library choice.

## Deviations from the spec

- CASE pin points down in the lib symbol, so the GND stub is below the APD (spec silent).
- D11 connects to HV_K, TIA_IN and GND by net labels, not wires (DNP part, keeps TIA_IN short).
- R50 value is written "10k" and C50 "10n" on the sheet for legibility; the 200 V / 500 V C0G ratings are in the footprint choice and this table.
- The 2 uC dump-risk note is in the sheet text only as an open test item.
