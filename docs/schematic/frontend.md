# Front End sheet as-built (`frontend.kicad_sch`, revision 2, decision 12)

Generated by `hardware/pcb/receiver-module/gen/frontend.py` (spec: `rev-a-sheet-specs.md` section 2; basis: `docs/research/post-amp-slicer.md`). Nothing here is simulated or measured; ERC and the kicad-cli netlist only confirm connectivity. Datasheets: OPA857 SBOS630D "OPA857"; OPA855 SBOS622D "OPA855" (printed page numbers). Labels: VERIFIED (datasheet cited), PROPOSED, UNRESOLVED.

Hierarchical pins: in APD_BIAS, 3V3, TIA_CTRL, VCM_REF; out SDET_P, TIA_TAP. SDET_N is built on the Slicer sheet. TIA_CTRL lands on the CTRL net (R57 pull-up, JP10).

## Signal path (left to right)
1. APD: R50 10k 1206 (200 V) from APD_BIAS to HV_K; C50 10n 1210 (500 V C0G) at the cathode; D10 S12023-10 (K = HV_K, A = TIA_IN, CASE = GND); D11 FDS010 DNP on the same nets. PROPOSED. Cathode to the positive bias and anode to IN is the OPA857 reference arrangement (OPA857 p.19, 8.2.1.2 step 2).
2. OPA857 TIA (U10): IN = TIA_IN; VS+ (9, 10, 11) = 3V3 with C56, C57 100n 0402 + C59 1u 0603; GND pins and PAD = GND; Test_SD (13) GND, Test_IN (14) 3V3 (OPA857 p.4 pin table); NC (16) open; CTRL = R57 100k pull-up to 3V3 (logic 1 = 20k) with JP10 (B = 3V3, A = GND, open by default). PROPOSED.
3. Coupling: C51 100n from TIA_OUT to PA_IN, R51 1k to VCM_REF (corner 1.6 kHz, 100 us). TIA_TAP = TIA_OUT through R58 1k. TIA_REF (OUTN) goes only to TP12.
4. OPA855 (U11), non-inverting, gain 1 + R53/R54 = 1 + 301/49.9 = 7.03. IN+ = PA_IN; RF R53 between FB (pin 1) and IN- (pin 3), drawn as a loop over the top; RG R54 from IN- to PA_REF; OUT (6) drives the load. PA_REF = VCM_REF through R60 10 ohm with C53 1u to GND. VS+ (7) = 3V3 with C52 100n 0402 + C58 1u 0603; VS- (5) and EP (9) = GND; PD (8) = 3V3 directly.
5. Low-pass: R56 49.9 + C54 47p = 68 MHz (PROPOSED; research asks 60-80 MHz).
6. C55 100n to SDET_P, R59 1k to VCM_REF. TP10 PA_OUT, TP11 SDET_P, TP12 TIA_REF.

## Datasheet facts that set values
- VERIFIED OPA855 p.17 (8.3.2, Fig. 8-4): FB (pin 1) is internally connected to the amplifier output; the feedback resistor goes between FB and IN- on the same side of the package; NC pin 2 must stay floating. OUT drives the load (the FB and OUT pins are two different external nets). Fig. 8-1 (p.16): non-inverting gain (1 + RF/RG) referenced to VREF.
- VERIFIED OPA855 p.7 (Power Down): PD low = off, high = on; enable threshold 1.5 V typ / 1.8 V max, disable below 1.0 V typ; PD bias current 70 uA typ / 140 uA max. The spec suggested 10k to the enabled level; 10k x 140 uA would drop 1.4 V and fail the 1.8 V max threshold, so PD is tied straight to 3V3, as the layout figure shows (p.29, 9.4.2: "Connect PD to VS+ to enable the amplifier").
- VERIFIED OPA855 p.20 (8.4.1) and p.29: thermal pad to VS- (GND here).
- VERIFIED OPA855 p.6: input common-mode 1.3 V max (low) to 2.7 V min (high) at VS+ = 3.3 V, so VCM_REF 1.65 V is inside; overdrive recovery 3 ns; IB -12 uA typ (-18.5 to -5).
- RF/RG: no RF/RG table exists. The datasheet's gain-7 test conditions use RF 453 ohm with RG about 75 ohm (Fig. 7-3, p.15; research doc). The spec's 301/49.9 (G = 7.03) is used: PROPOSED. UNRESOLVED: stability with RF 301 at G = 7 not checked; the research note's alternative is 453/75.
- VERIFIED OPA857 p.15 (7.3.1): class-A output swings from the 1.83 V common-mode level toward the negative rail only; 1.2 V swing on 3.3 V; 60 uA at 20k, 240 uA at 5k.
- **OPA857 output polarity (VERIFIED, p.19, 8.2.1.2 step 2):** with the APD cathode on positive bias and anode on IN, photocurrent into IN makes OUT swing DOWN below the reference OUTN (1.83 V). Consequences: (a) the Monitor sheet's RSSI peak detector on TIA_TAP must track the minimum (negative peaks) of the waveform, relative to a DC level that is TIA_OUT's idle (about 1.83 V before AC coupling); (b) after the non-inverting OPA855 and AC coupling, "light on" is BELOW the VCM_REF baseline on SDET_P, so LVDS_P is low for a one: the FPGA must invert or swap the pair.

## PROPOSED design values
- Output offset: IB -12 uA into R51 1k gives about +12 mV at IN+, x7.03 = +84 mV, minus IB x RF = -3.6 mV, so about +80 mV (derived): idle about 1.73 V, near the 1.75 V mid-point of the output limits 1.15 to 2.35 V (OPA855 p.7). Output is AC coupled afterwards, so only headroom is affected.
- Clip level (derived): 1.15 V / (7 x 18.2k) = 9 uA at 20k; 36.5 uA at 5k (research section 3); the TIA itself saturates at 60 / 240 uA.
- R60 10 ohm isolates the 1u PA_REF decoupling cap from the Monitor sheet's VCM_REF buffer (added part; not in the spec).
