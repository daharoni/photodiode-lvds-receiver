## Layout
Top-left block (revision 3): U5 TPS2121 source mux with its PR1/OV1/OV2 dividers (R35/R36, R39/R40, R41/R42), C33/C34 input caps, R37 ST pull-up, R38 ILM, C32 SS; hierarchical inputs +5V_CABLE and VBUS, output PWR_SRC. Top band: +5V (U5 OUT) caps, U1 3V3 and U4 3V3_D side by side; local label +5V replaces the former hierarchical input. Bottom band (moved up 60.96 mm after the LM27762 block was removed): U3 controller (left), power stage and doubler (centre), clamp, filter stage 1 and APD_BIAS (right), HV_MON chain (top right), support networks (EN divider + Q3 gate / J1, SYNC, BIAS, RT, SS, VC, DAC leg, FBX divider, pot leg) in two rows underneath. Local labels: 3V3_D (pull-up R33), V3_FB, HV_V1, HV_PUMP, HV_RAW, HV_OUT, HV_CLAMP, 5V_HVIN, EN, SYNC, BIAS, RT, SS, VC, FBX. APD_BIAS is the HV_FILT node.

## ERC
Revision 3 root ERC (kicad-cli 10.0.5, 2026-10-09): 0 errors, 0 warnings. The lab-library TPS2121 symbol originally had both OUT pins (1 and 8) as power_out, which gave one `pin_to_pin` error with them paralleled on +5V; lab-library PR #60 makes pin 8 passive (pin 1 stays power_out) and the sheets were regenerated against that symbol. Because +5V has a power_out driver, no PWR_FLAG is needed on +5V; the root PWR_FLAG #FLG04 moved to the +5V_CABLE net and #FLG60 on VBUS stays in the Controller sheet. SW60/SW61 resolve only if the global sym-lib-table and fp-lib-table carry the AharoniLab_Switch / AharoniLab_Switch_SMD rows from the lab library tables. Not measured or simulated.

## Revision 2 (decision 12, 2026-10-08)
Removed: U2 LM27762 (+3V7/-3V7), C13-C17, R3-R6, R31, TP1 (PG_VGA), the 5V_A bead FB1 with C3/C4 and its PWR_FLAG, and the hierarchical outputs +3V7, -3V7, 5V_A, PGOOD_VGA. Hierarchical pins now: in +5V, DAC_HV, HV_DIS; out 3V3, 3V3_D, APD_BIAS, HV_MON.
3V3 load estimate: OPA857 26.8 mA max + OPA855 19.5 mA max + TLV3605 17.5 mA max + OPA2320 about 2 mA = about 66 mA, against the TPS79901 200 mA rating (TPS rating not rechecked here). OPA855 IQ: 17.8 mA typ, 16 to 19.5 mA over production limits at VS = 5 V (OPA855 SBOS622D PDF p.7, Electrical Characteristics, POWER SUPPLY); Fig. 6-27 (PDF p.12) shows about 16 to 17.5 mA across 3 to 5.25 V, so 19.5 mA is conservative. Estimate, not measured: PROPOSED.

## Revision 3 (decision 13, 2026-10-09)
Added U5 TPS2121RUXR with R35-R42, C32-C34 (see the values table). The +5V hierarchical input is gone: +5V is the internal merged rail (U5 OUT) feeding U1, U4 and U3 via L2; C1/C2 stay on it. Hierarchical pins now: in +5V_CABLE, VBUS, DAC_HV, HV_DIS; out 3V3, 3V3_D, APD_BIAS, HV_MON, PWR_SRC. Budget about 0.19 A worst case (0.24 A with margin) from +5V against 0.5 A USB default and the 1.24 A typ limit (docs/research/usb-standalone-power.md section 1.5; estimate with assumed 70 % LT8365 efficiency, not measured); radio must stay disabled. No back-feed (fast reverse blocking, <= 1 uA leakage, MUX p.7, p.15); switchover about 100 us typ (p.7), hold-up droop about 1 V estimated, not measured: PROPOSED. UNRESOLVED: whether a bus-powered hub or unconfigured device is held to 100 mA; USB-alone HV start must be stepped from the low-bias end (a faulted start can trip a USB port).
