# Slicer sheet as-built (`slicer.kicad_sch`, revision 2, decision 12)

Generated by `hardware/pcb/receiver-module/gen/slicer.py` (spec: `rev-a-sheet-specs.md` section 3; basis `docs/research/post-amp-slicer.md`). Nothing here is simulated or measured. Datasheet: TLV3604/3605/3607, SNOSDA2F, "DS" below (printed pages).

Hierarchical pins: in SDET_P, VCM_REF, DAC_TH, 3V3, SHDN_N, LE_HYS; out LVDS_P, LVDS_N, VTH_MON.

## Circuit
- IN+ (4) = SDET_P (already AC coupled and returned to VCM_REF through R59 1k on the Front End sheet). IN- (6) = SDET_N: R84 1k from VCM_REF; DAC_TH through R85 100k; C84 10n to GND; VTH_MON through R86 10k; TP20 (SDET_N). TP21 SDET_P is DNP.
- Trim authority (derived): (DAC - VCM_REF) x 1k/101k; 0 to 3.3 V with VCM_REF 1.65 V gives +-16.3 mV, 8 uV per 12-bit LSB. R84 matches the 1k on IN+ so bias currents cancel: IB -1 uA typ / +-5 uA max, IOS +-1 uA (DS p.7); research section 1.
- External hysteresis (PROPOSED): R87 5.49k in series with C87 100p from OUT+ to IN+. OUT+ swings 350 mVpp (VOD 350 mV typ, 250-450; VOCM 1.2 V, 1.125-1.375; DS p.7; LVDS into the FPGA's 100 ohm, `docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md`). Source at IN+ during an edge = R56 49.9 || R59 1k = 47.5 ohm (the coupling cap is a short at 50 MHz). Window = 350 mV x 47.5/(5490 + 47.5) = 3.0 mV. C87 is added because a bare resistor would also feed the DC level: (1.2 - 1.65) x 1k/(1k + 5.49k) = -69 mV static shift of IN+. With C87: corner 1/(2 pi x 5.54k x 100p) = 287 kHz, tau 554 ns, 83 % of the window left after a 100 ns run (5 UI). The spec's "about 100k with a 1k source" is the slow-state value only: it gives 3.5 mV in held states but 0.17 mV at 50 MHz edges, so it was not used. UNRESOLVED: jitter and noise effect not simulated.
- LE/HYS (8) is left open (internal hysteresis 0 mV, latch off; DS p.8); the smallest characterised internal setting is about 8 mV (Fig. 7-4, p.16). R82 150k to GND and R83 0R to LE_HYS are DNP footprints.
- SHDN (7): R81 10k to 3V3, SHDN_N active low (VIH 1.5 V, VIL 0.4 V, DS p.8); shutdown is not a guaranteed zero output (standing correction 11).
- VCCI (2) = 3V3 with C80 100n 0402 + C81 1u; VCCO (1) = 3V3 through R80 0R with C82 100n 0402 + C83 1u; PWR_FLAG on the VCCO net (R80 is passive); supply 2.4 to 5.5 V (DS p.19). VEE and EP = GND (no pad requirement given beyond the ground plane).
- Polarity: light on pulls SDET_P below the baseline (OPA857 output swings down, SBOS630D p.19), so LVDS_P is low for a one; invert or swap pairs at the FPGA.
