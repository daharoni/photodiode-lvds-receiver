## Datasheet facts used

- VERIFIED: supply 2.4 V to 5.5 V with separate VCCI and VCCO (DS p.19, 7.3). SHDN and LE/HYS are 1.8 V logic compatible regardless of VCCO (DS p.19, 7.3; p.13, 6.4).
- VERIFIED: place a 0.1 uF ceramic directly between VCCI/VCCO and VEE; 100 ohm termination across the LVDS outputs; matched-length input and output traces; solid ground plane (DS p.19, 7.4.1 items 1, 2, 3, 6). The 1 uF caps are a design addition, not a datasheet requirement.
- VERIFIED: SHDN is active low, pin 7; VIH_SD 1.5 V min, VIL_SD 0.4 V max, IIL_SD 30 uA max (DS p.8, EC table). Shutdown is not a guaranteed zero output (standing correction 11).
- VERIFIED: latch and hysteresis share pin 8 (DS p.3, Table 4-1). The output is held (latched) when the pin is less than 800 mV above VEE (DS p.14, 7.1.3); EC table gives VIL_LE 0.35 V max and VIH_LE 1.5 V min, IIL_LE 40 uA max (p.8). A resistor from the pin to VEE sets hysteresis: floating 0 mV, 150k 30 mV, 56k 60 mV (DS p.8 EC table; p.15, 7.1.4, Figure 7-4).
- VERIFIED: pin 13 is the exposed pad of the RVK package (symbol); the datasheet text gives no pad connection requirement beyond the ground-plane guideline. EP is tied to GND here (PROPOSED).
- Thermal: RthetaJA 85.8 C/W for RVK (DS p.6, 5.4).

## Notes and open items

- UNRESOLVED: the DC voltage on LE/HYS with a 150k resistor fitted is not in the datasheet. It must stay above the latch threshold (VIH_LE) in normal operation; verify on the bench (G2). The 150k value is the EC-table test condition, so it is the expected non-latching setting.
- UNRESOLVED: if R83 is fitted, the FPGA must pull LE_HYS low only (open drain or tri-state otherwise). Driving it high may override the hysteresis setting. Latch use is policy-dependent (decision-log standing correction 11).
- R82 footprint should allow 56k (60 mV) by value change only; no extra pads.
- PROPOSED: the SDET and VTH wires are 2.54 mm apart on the sheet; this is schematic spacing only and carries no layout meaning.
- No LVDS termination on this board; ECP5 on-die 100 ohm (`docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md`).
