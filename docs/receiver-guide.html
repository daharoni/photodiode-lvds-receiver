01 · Start here

What the module is

An optical receiver module for the next-generation wireless UCLA Miniscope data uplink. A silicon avalanche photodiode turns the light stream into current, a fast analog chain turns it into one LVDS pair, and that pair travels down a USB-C cable to an ECP5 FPGA (ULX3S first). Each module also carries a small ESP32-C6 that does slow housekeeping only.

Line rate is 50 Mbps with 8b/10b coding (UI = 20 ns, 40 Mbps payload). Performance beats cost. No backward compatibility with the Miniscope Zero headstage is required; the headstage emitter, driver and encoder are co-designed with this receiver. PROPOSED

Hard rule: analog first

Slicing is analog and the module decodes with the ESP32 unprogrammed. The ESP32-C6 is not in the signal path. It does slow range trim (APD bias DAC, TIA 20k/5k select), monitoring, HV gating, module identity and the CAN link. This is a firm user decision (decision 1, read through decision 12). Do not propose an MCU/ADC-first architecture.

02 · Signal chain

From photons to one LVDS pair

Every block is there for a reason that comes from the one before it. The diode is a current source with large capacitance, so it gets a transimpedance stage first. Ambient light would be multiplied by the gain, so everything after the TIA is AC coupled. The comparator needs more than a few millivolts of signal to ignore its own offset, so a fixed-gain amplifier sits ahead of it.

2.1 APD and its bias filter

Why an APD. Photocurrent into a bare FDS010 PIN is about 1-3 nA, far below the TIA's noise (about 8-26 nA), so a bare PIN was 18-23 dB optical short of Q = 6 at 35 cm lateral with any emitter that fits the headstage power budget. Etendue limits optics to about 2x for a +/-45 degree field of view. An APD with gain M plus a modestly brighter NIR emitter closes the link (decision 8, link-budget.md; calculated from LED datasheets, no received power measured). PROPOSED

  • Primary: Hamamatsu S12023-10, phi 1.0 mm, 6 pF, VBR 150 V typ / 200 V max, operating about 120-190 V for M = 50-100, dVBR/dT about 0.65 V/C, TO-18 with window. Fallback: S12023-05, phi 0.5 mm, 2 pF. The FDS010 is bench-only and is not on the board. PROPOSED Per-unit operating voltage is UNRESOLVED until G1.
  • Use 6 pF for the S12023-10 and the FDS010 alike (standing correction 3: FDS010 6 pF typ at 10 V, VERIFIED Thorlabs 0636-S01 Rev I p.1).
  • Polarity: cathode to the positive bias through R50 10k (series current limit) and C50 10 nF (500 V C0G, at the cathode); anode to the OPA857 IN. Reverse bias is about cathode voltage minus 1.83 V. This is the OPA857 reference arrangement VERIFIED SBOS630D p.19, 8.2.1.2 step 2.
  • The HV node needs 200 V creepage and clearance and a keep-out from the TIA input. The anode-to-IN trace is the shortest on the board with no HV copper near it.
  • Ambient light is multiplied by M, so the optical bandpass filter is mandatory and the OPA857 20k linear range (60 uA at the input) is the saturation bound.

2.2 OPA857 transimpedance stage

Why first. The diode is a current source with large capacitance. Converting to voltage at the source keeps noise and bandwidth under control before any gain stage.

  • CTRL selects 20k or 5k feedback. A 100 k pull-up (R57) to 3V3 gives logic 1 = 20k, so an unprogrammed module runs at 20k. JP10 is an open solder jumper. Single-ended transimpedance is 18.2k at 20k VERIFIED SBOS630D p.6.
  • Output is class A and swings from the 1.83 V reference (OUTN, 5/9 of 3.3 V) toward the negative rail only: 1.2 V swing, 60 uA at 20k, 240 uA at 5k VERIFIED SBOS630D p.15, 7.3.1.
  • Polarity. With the cathode on positive bias and the anode on IN, photocurrent makes OUT swing down below OUTN VERIFIED SBOS630D p.19. Light on is therefore below the baseline after the non-inverting post-amp. LVDS_P is low for a logic one. See the polarity subsection of section 04 for what that means at the connector.
  • 6 pF is not the datasheet's case. SBOS630D characterises 1.5 pF only. SIMULATED At 6 pF the 20k mode shows 0.97 dB peaking at 54 MHz, 13.8 % overshoot on a 5-UI run and 12.6 ns to settle to 2 % of a 20 ns UI. The 5k mode is clean (1.7 %, 4.7 ns). So 5k is preferred whenever the signal allows. The 20k eye at 6 pF is the key G1 measurement. UNRESOLVED

2.3 First AC coupling

OUTN sits at 1.83 V. A DC-coupled gain stage would multiply ambient photocurrent and offset. C51 100 nF into R51 1k to VCM_REF (corner about 1.6 kHz) removes the DC and ambient part at the price of baseline wander on long runs. 8b/10b bounds run length and DC balance, which is why this is acceptable. 6.63 MHz field pickup is handled by shielding, not filtering. PROPOSED

2.4 OPA855 fixed-gain post-amp

Why it exists. At the operating point the TIA delivers 15-25 mVpp. The TLV3605 has up to +/-5 mV offset and its datasheet gives no input noise or small-overdrive behaviour, so a direct connection cannot be proven from the datasheet VERIFIED SNOSDA2F p.7 (absence of noise data). Gain x7 lifts 15-25 mVpp to 105-175 mVpp, which makes offset and unspecified noise stop mattering. PROPOSED

  • Non-inverting, gain 1 + 301/49.9 = 7.03, single 3.3 V supply. x7 is the OPA855 minimum stable gain. PA_REF is VCM_REF through R60 10 ohm with C53 1 uF. PD is tied to 3V3.
  • FB (pin 1) is internally the output; the feedback resistor goes between FB and IN- on the same side of the package VERIFIED SBOS622D p.17, 8.3.2. Input common-mode range 1.3 V max low to 2.7 V min high at 3.3 V, so VCM_REF = 1.65 V is inside VERIFIED p.6. Overdrive recovery 3 ns VERIFIED p.6.
  • The datasheet's gain-7 test values are RF 453 / RG about 75 (Fig. 7-3, p.15); the sheet uses 301 / 49.9. SIMULATED The two behave the same at gain 7 in the model: 77 degrees phase margin, 0.1 dB peaking. Stability as built is UNRESOLVED until G1.
  • Idle output level: derived 1.73 V, model 1.578 V, neither verified. It matters only for headroom. UNRESOLVED

2.5 Low-pass and second AC coupling

R56 49.9 ohm with C54 47 pF gives about 68 MHz (research asks 60-80 MHz). The 20 ns UI needs about 35 MHz, so this trims noise without eating the eye. C55 100 nF and R59 1k to VCM_REF set the comparator input node SDET_P at the 1.65 V baseline. PROPOSED

2.6 VCM_REF and RSSI (Monitor sheet)

  • VCM_REF. OPA2320 half U30A buffers a 10k/10k divider to 1.65 V through R102 10 ohm into C101 1 uF. It is the common baseline for every AC-coupled stage after the TIA. With DC-balanced 8b/10b, the eye centre is that baseline, so no threshold tracking is needed. PROPOSED SIMULATED The 10 ohm isolation resistor gives 87.6 degrees phase margin and 0.96 mV of VCM_REF shift for a 100 mV disturbance through 1 k; without it the model has about 1 degree of margin. The real board is UNRESOLVED until the G1 step test.
  • RSSI. OPA2320 half U30B behind a BAS70 (D30, cathode on TIA_TAP) tracks the negative peaks of the TIA output; 1 nF with 1 M to 3V3 gives tau 1 ms. RSSI falls as light rises and idles near 1.9-2.0 V. It is a slow relative indicator for the ESP32, not a measurement, and it cannot separate signal from ambient. PROPOSED

2.7 TLV3605 comparator

  • IN+ = SDET_P. IN- = SDET_N = VCM_REF through R84 1k, with DAC_TH trimmed in through R85 100k: (DAC - VCM_REF) x 1k/101k gives +/-16.3 mV, 8 uV per 12-bit LSB. R84 matches the 1k on IN+ so the bias currents cancel (IB -1 uA typ, +/-5 uA max) VERIFIED SNOSDA2F p.7; trim range calculated.
  • External hysteresis: R87 5.49k in series with C87 100 pF from OUT+ to IN+ gives about 3.0 mV at the edges. The smallest characterised internal setting is about 8 mV VERIFIED SNOSDA2F p.16 Fig. 7-4, too large for a 15-25 mVpp signal without gain. LE/HYS is left open. PROPOSED Jitter and noise effect not simulated. UNRESOLVED
  • SHDN is active low with a 10k pull-up (SHDN_N). Shutdown is not a guaranteed zero output (standing correction 11). Output is LVDS: VOD 350 mV typ (250-450), VOCM 1.2 V VERIFIED SNOSDA2F p.7.
  • Comparator noise and delay below 10 mV overdrive: the datasheet has none. UNRESOLVED The G1 small-signal test measures it.

2.8 LVDS fan-out

The TLV3605 drives an SN65LVDS104 (U50) with a 100 ohm termination R180 across its input (no on-chip termination, VERIFIED SLLS396G p.16 Fig. 25). Margin: VOD 250 mV minimum against the buffer's +/-100 mV threshold leaves 150 mV VERIFIED arithmetic from SNOSDA2F p.7 and SLLS396G p.4-5. Outputs 1 and 2 feed the two SuperSpeed TX pairs of J50, output 3 goes to the J51 scope header, output 4 is disabled. Driving both pairs straight from the comparator was rejected: a 50 ohm load, about 125 mV VOD at minimum, and a hysteresis window that depends on whether a cable is plugged in. PROPOSED Details in section 04.

2.10 One setting, plus firmware, covers the range

Decision 12 states that one fixed setting covers about 39 dB of signal range and firmware adds about 32 dB: lowering APD gain from M = 50 to M = 5 buys 20 dB of input current, and switching 20k to 5k buys 12 dB (18.2k / 4.5k = 4x), 32 dB together. The photocurrent spread across the arena (about 6-15 dB between on-axis and 35 cm lateral at 50 cm height, estimated) fits inside the window, so one setting per session plus slow M tracking could work. Daniel's argument against an analog AGC: received intensity changes only as fast as the animal moves, which is slow compared with a firmware loop of tens of ms. PROPOSED The 39 dB window and the clip behaviour are tested at G2 (BER against photocurrent at fixed settings). UNRESOLVED

3.3 APD bias: LT8365 boost plus charge-pump doubler

Why this topology. A boost at 5 V cannot do 220 V alone (150 V switch maximum, LT8365 Rev. A p.2). Boosting to V1 of about (Vout + diode drop)/2, 70 V at 140 V out and 110 V at 220 V out, and doubling once keeps the switch node well under 150 V (17 V margin at 220 V after a 20 % ring allowance, an assumption), and means a short on the output never creates a DC path through the inductor and diode. The datasheet frequency limit is 500 kHz (RT 84.5k), so the 1 MHz first asked for is not available; attenuation goes into the filter instead. At 1 mA or less the part runs in deep discontinuous mode and pulse-skips. This follows the Analog Devices DC2838A demo circuit (manual Rev. 0) and the 5 V-in precedent on LT8365 datasheet p.30, with the topology traced by eye from images. PROPOSED

  • L1 10 uH (Wurth 74437324100), D1, D3, D4 150 V Schottky (D2 is fitted DNP, purpose not stated in LT8365 p.30), 0.1 uF 200 V pump capacitors, SYNC floating (pulse-skip; footprints for Burst options), BIAS tied to GND, SS 0.22 uF, INTVCC 1 uF. VERIFIED SYNC/MODE table and BIAS rule, LT8365 Rev. A p.7; the choices are PROPOSED.
  • Output set by the FBX divider: four 2.05 M resistors, R12 86.6k with a 10k NTC (TH1) for temperature tracking, a DAC leg (DAC_HV through R13 10k, R14 442k) for firmware trim, and a DNP pot leg. The NTC tracks about 0.64 V/C against the APD's 0.65 V/C with +/-0.7 V residual over 15-35 C. calculated PROPOSED
  • DAC_HV polarity: 0 V gives the maximum bias (about 215 V) and full scale gives the minimum (about 104 V with the trimmer at default). The MCP4728 factory EEPROM is 0 V, so an unprogrammed DAC means maximum bias. Hence HV stays disabled until the ESP32 verifies the DAC. calculated
  • Open risk: the C50 capacitor dump into a sacrificial TIA at an HV fault (C50 10 nF versus 4.7 nF). Steady hard-short current into the TIA is 5.95 mA at 220 V through R50 plus the upstream resistor against the OPA857 abs max of 10 mA (VERIFIED SBOS630D p.5); with the clamp upstream it is about 0.17 mA. Stored energy at 220 V is about 2.3 mJ. All calculated; the G1 discharge test decides.

3.4 Filter, clamp and monitor

  • Two-stage RC filter. Stage 1 on the Power sheet is R19 27k with C8 10 nF (500 V C0G); stage 2 at the cathode is R50 10k with C50 10 nF. Calculated attenuation about 108 dB at 500 kHz and 142 dB at 6.63 MHz with parasitics, cathode noise about 1.4 uV rms against a 10 uV target (assumes a 1 Vpp source). calculated PROPOSED
  • Clamp. Q1 MMBTA42 and Q2 MMBT3904 with R16 4.02k and 2 x 4.99 M limit the current an HV fault can push toward the TIA. Topology checked in the netlist. PROPOSED
  • HV_MON. 2 x 4.99 M over R21 100k, C28 10 nF: HV_MON = HV_OUT/101. It is the only HV observation point. There is no bare APD_BIAS pad.

3.6 HV safety rules

  • APD bias is 100-220 V. Layout uses 200 V creepage and clearance; net class HV is 1.0 mm clearance (HV_LADDER 0.6 mm for the 4.99 M and 2.05 M resistor ladders, steps of about 55 V).
  • Every net at or above 50 V is prefixed HV_. HV_MON is the only observation point; no bare APD_BIAS test pad.
  • Series current limit (R50, plus the clamp) and a keep-out from the TIA input, including inner layers.
  • HV is off until the ESP32 drives HV_DIS low. In USB-alone mode the firmware starts HV from the low-bias end of the DAC in steps, because a faulted HV start can trip a USB port.
  • HV outer layers only; GND1 and GND2 stay solid under HV traces (no plane cuts).

04 · Connector, cable and LVDS fan-out

One 24-pin USB-C, with the LVDS on both SuperSpeed TX pairs

Decision 14 (2026-10-09, approved by Daniel, PROPOSED until G1 and G4) replaced the 12-pin system header and the 16-pin USB-C with one 24-pin full-featured USB-C receptacle, Wuerth 632723300011, horizontal. One connector and one standard cable per module. Plug orientation must not cost ECP5 LVDS pairs. USB stays a line to a computer; between the FPGA and the modules the system picture is CAN plus LVDS.

History. Decision 14 removed the 12-pin J50 header, the 16-pin USB-C J60, the TPS2121 two-source mux, +5V_CABLE, PWR_SRC, the VBUS_SENSE divider and AUX2. Decision 13's requirement (a laptop can program and verify a module over USB alone) is kept.

4.2 Why a fan-out buffer

Both TX pairs carry the same signal so the module works in either plug orientation. U50 (SN65LVDS104PW) repeats the single TLV3605 output onto both pairs and J51. It is powered from 3V3 (23 mA typ, 35 mA max, VERIFIED SLLS396G p.5), enables active high (EN1-EN3 to 3V3, EN4 to GND, VERIFIED section 9.4 Table 2), and drives a static high if the TLV3605 is unpowered (VERIFIED p.15-16). R180 100 ohm 0.1 % at its input is the module's termination; the ECP5 end keeps its on-die DIFFRESISTOR. The 90 ohm pairs run on the top layer over GND1 with intra-pair match within 1 mm (routing target, PROPOSED).

4.3 Polarity, end to end

  1. Light on makes the OPA857 output swing down, so SDET_P falls below the VCM_REF baseline and LVDS_P is low for a logic one (VERIFIED SBOS630D p.19 for the swing direction).
  2. On 2026-10-09 Daniel flipped the polarity at J50 on both pairs: N goes to SSTXp and P to SSTXn. The same day U50 output 1 was assigned to the upper end of J50 (B2/B3) and output 2 to the lower end (A2/A3) so the two pairs reach their ESD clamps without crossing.
  3. The consequence: the cable carries the fan-out output inverted, and SSTXp is HIGH for a logic one at the module's J50.
  4. The FPGA-end inversion is a gateware constant. It belongs in the breakout and gateware docs, which do not exist yet. PROPOSED

4.4 Orientation does not matter

A standard full-featured C-to-C cable maps TX1 to RX1 and TX2 to RX2, p to p and n to n, so a flip at either end swaps TX1/TX2 and RX1/RX2 with polarity preserved (VERIFIED cable wiring, TC R2.5 Table 3-10 p.83; the analysis is PROPOSED). Because the module drives both TX pairs identically and receives CAN on both RX pairs, in all four flip combinations the module LVDS lands on the breakout's RX1 and RX2 and the breakout CAN lands on both module RX pairs. The breakout takes LVDS from RX1 alone and terminates RX2 with 100 ohm. SBU1 tied to SBU2 at both ends makes the SBU swap irrelevant.

4.5 CC sense, SBU and the foreign-host problem

  • CC. Rd 5.1k on each CC pin detects the source's advertisement (VERIFIED USB Type-C R2.0 Table 4-25 p.236). The 100k/100k taps keep the ESP32 pin under 3.3 V even if an e-marked cable puts 5.5 V VCONN on the unused CC pin (2.75 V at the pin). The firmware logs class and orientation as hints only and never clears HV_DIS on CC alone. ADC classification margin is thin: about +/-46 mV at the CC pin against class-edge margins of about 20-25 mV. UNRESOLVED
  • SBU / AUX. One slow single-ended line (about 1 MHz or less) between the module and the FPGA master, used to map CAN nodes to LVDS lanes. A driven AUX line departs from the Type-C rule that SBU stays open or weakly pulled down; this is accepted and UNRESOLVED. The firmware keeps AUX high-Z until LINK_ALL or the host class is known. A laptop in DisplayPort alternate mode might drive SBU. UNRESOLVED
  • Laptop plugged in directly. A laptop host may see the CAN transceiver load (10-25 kohm per pin) and the LVDS on its SuperSpeed pins. Expected harmless, never tested. Mitigations are held in reserve: AC-couple the LVDS TX pins with 100 nF, or hold U50 disabled until VBUS and CC identify a known partner. The CAN TX stays silent until bus traffic or the FPGA master is seen. UNRESOLVED G4.

4.6 ESD arrays split by position

U51 and U52 are TPD6E05U06 (0.47 pF typ, VRWM 5.5 V, VERIFIED TPD Table 5.4 p.7; place within a few mm of J50). A 2026-10-09 hand edit changed the split from by-function to by-position along the J50 pad rows. U51 clamps the lower-end pins (A5 CC1, A3/A2 LVDS pair 2, B10/B11 CAN) and U52 the upper-end pins (B2/B3 LVDS pair 1, A11/A10 CAN, B5 CC2, A8/B8 SBU). Reason: the two plug orientations put the same function at opposite ends of the receptacle, and the old split made half the protected lines run the length of the pad rows. A-row SMD pads get straight top-layer traces; B-row through-hole pins arrive on an inner layer with a via. Nets are unchanged; the ERC stays 0/0. PROPOSED Open: the unidirectional clamps conduct near -0.7 V, so CAN common-mode excursion with up to 0.4 V of cable ground drop needs a check. UNRESOLVED

  • Cable rule (decision 14, PROPOSED). Passive full-featured USB-C cable, USB 3.2 Gen 1, 3 A, e-marked. 5.3 When the ESP32 is not programmed

  • TIA_CTRL floats and the pull-up gives 20k. SHDN_N is pulled up so the comparator is on. The module decodes whatever light arrives.
  • No APD bias unless bench jumper J1 is fitted, because HV_DIS floats high through R33. With J1 fitted and an unprogrammed DAC the bias goes to about 215 V; firmware cannot prevent that.
  • DAC_TH comes up at the MCP4728 EEPROM default (0 V on every channel from the factory), a safe-default direction that is UNRESOLVED for DAC_TH.

5.4 Boot, in order

  1. First instruction: write HV_DIS = 1, then enable the pin as an output. Read the reset reason; after a brown-out or watchdog reset HV stays off until a deliberate command.
  2. Load and clamp the stored parameters (hv_min at least 100 V, default hv_max 200 V, absolute 220 V).
  3. Recover and probe I2C. If the DAC or ADC is missing, HV stays off but console and CAN still start.
  4. Verify the MCP4728 EEPROM with the Read command (channel C must read VREF = VDD, code 0xFFF, minimum bias; channel B 0x800). The General Call Read named in older notes only returns address bits (DS22187E 5.4.4 p.32-33, VERIFIED). A guarded console command provisions the defaults.
  5. Force live outputs to safe values, check the ADC with HV off (HV_MON should read about 0.05 V; more than 30 V of bias while disabled sets HV_STUCK).
  6. Read CC1/CC2 and watch for USB SOF, then start CAN in listen-only for 1.5 s or more to find the mode.
  7. HV stays off until commanded: console hv on (standalone), master hv_cmd over CAN, or a stored hv_auto flag with a heard master and no faults (default 0).
  8. Soft start: first write DAC_HV = 0xFFF and read it back (the only path that lowers HV_DIS), then drive HV_DIS low, let the LT8365 soft-start (SS 0.22 uF) reach the DAC minimum of about 104-110 V, then walk DAC_HV down closed-loop on HV_MON at no more than 20 V/s with a code bound derived from hv_max and a trip if the bias does not follow the code. Timeout 10 s gives HV_UNDER and HV off.

Any ESP32 reset, crash or loss of 3V3_D releases IO0, so HV turns off with no firmware action. HV also goes off within 10 ms of: I2C or ADC failure, HV out of range, CAN bus-off or no LINK_ALL for the 2 s default in bus mode. PROPOSED

06 · FPGA side

What the ECP5 end has to do

The module sends no clock. The FPGA recovers it. Nothing in firmware/ecp5/ exists yet and the breakout board is not designed, so this section is the contract the module assumes. Items are PROPOSED unless labelled.

6.1 ULX3S input pairs

  • True differential inputs: gp/gn[8..13] (bank 7, VCCIO jumper-selectable 2.5 or 3.3 V) and gp/gn[14..21] (banks 2/3, 3.3 V). That is 14 pairs, one per module. Banks 0/1 (gp/gn[0..7], [22..27]) have no differential inputs. VERIFIED ULX3S v3.1.7 schematic p.2-3; Lattice FPGA-TN-02032 v1.4 p.14, p.26.
  • ECP5 differential inputs are powered from VCCAUX, independent of VCCIO, and on-die DIFFRESISTOR = 100 exists on the left and right banks, so the pair goes straight into the pins with no receiver IC and no external termination. VERIFIED FPGA-TN-02032 v1.4 s.4.3, s.4.10, s.4.11.8; see research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md. This closed the earlier open item about LVDS on a 3.3 V bank.
  • The ECP5 input threshold is +/-100 mV (ECP5 DS Table 3.13 p.58), against at least 250 mV VOD from the module's fan-out. VERIFIED for the datasheet figures.
  • Still open: ECP5 input common-mode range against the module output's common mode (family datasheet value not retrieved), and DIFFRESISTOR support in nextpnr. UNRESOLVED

6.2 Gateware duties

  • Clock and data recovery in the fabric. Reason: no clock is sent, and a digital CDR is flexible, lets the rate change and avoids a PLL on the module.
  • 8b/10b decode and framing. Framed packets with sequence numbers; commas regularly in a continuous stream (assumed, burst and idle structure is open).
  • Redundant-lane merge. Several receivers see the same stream; the FPGA selects validated copies. Maximum skew and merge policy are not specified. UNRESOLVED
  • Link validity. Decoder validity is the LOS judge for the whole system. It feeds LINK_ALL to every module's firmware.

Watchdog rule (standing correction 12)

The FPGA watchdog runs on an independent local clock, not on the recovered one, so it survives loss of the recovered clock. After release the CDR and framing are reacquired. Both constant LVDS states are "invalid" after a timeout (correction 11: TLV3605 shutdown is not a guaranteed zero and a latched comparator holds its last state). Invalid-code counts are not a BER measurement because they miss errors.

6.3 Polarity constant

At the module's J50, SSTXp is HIGH for a logic one on both pairs (section 04.3). The FPGA end applies a fixed inversion or pair swap in gateware or on the breakout. It is one constant, the same on every lane, and plug orientation never changes it. It must be recorded in the breakout and gateware docs when they exist. PROPOSED

6.4 Breakout board concept (not designed)

  • One Type-C source port per module. 5 V per port is applied only after Rd is seen. VCONN for the e-marked cable. Advertised 1.5 A.
  • RX1 wired to the ECP5 LVDS input, RX2 terminated 100 ohm. The breakout's own CAN on TX1/TX2 onto a shared bus with the split termination. D+/D- into a USB hub so one computer sees every module console.
  • The ULX3S cannot be a USB host. The FPGA board connects by LVDS and CAN only. Brick part, per-port TPS25810 design, VCONN routing and the hub are undone. UNRESOLVED

6.5 Host link (open)

UNRESOLVED, a separate decision. How the FPGA end reaches a computer is not decided. Daniel's preference to evaluate first (2026-10-09, PROPOSED): the Cypress/Infineon FX3 (CYUSB3014 family, USB 3.0 device controller with a parallel GPIF II bus, known from the Miniscope DAQ) on the ULX3S GPIO header. 09 · Bring-up gates

Four gates, none started

From bringup/gates.md. Goals marked "goal" are provisional, not user-approved. Gate names follow gates.md (the authority); CLAUDE.md still carries the older G2 and G3 names. Do not order PCBs before the G4 pre-order checks pass. Checklist items are static here; the working checklist lives in gates.md.

G1: fast-path proof

Acceptance: the manual-gain optical eye is open at the target rate across the bias range and both TIA gains; supplies meet worst-case needs; the TIA-to-post-amp interface is within limits. Compare each measurement with the simulated number named in its item.

APD and bias

  • VBR and gain-vs-bias per unit at room temperature (S12023-10; record the voltage for M = 50 and 100)
  • Bias ripple and noise at the APD node with the TIA running; compare SYNC float (pulse-skip) with Burst
  • Cathode-capacitor discharge into a sacrificial TIA (C50 10 nF versus 4.7 nF)
  • Bias temperature tracking over 15-35 C
  • Ambient saturation margin with the real optical filter (20k, 60 uA)
  • Record wavelength and optical power

TIA, post-amp, slicer

  • Optical eye, step and saturation across the bias range and both TIA gains at fixed post-amp gain
  • Post-amp clip test: 2x and 10x over the 9 uA (20k) clip with a 5-UI pattern; recovery and zero-crossing shift against 3 ns; look for oscillation while overdriven and the real clip near 1.05-1.15 V
  • OPA855 stability as built (RF 301 / RG 49.9); compare 0.1 dB peaking, 2.13 GHz, about 1 % overshoot; idle level against 1.578 V and 1.73 V
  • OPA857 at 6 pF in both modes: bandwidth, peaking and the 50 Mb/s eye; compare 20k 13.8 % / 12.6 ns and 5k 1.7 % / 4.7 ns. The 20k eye is the key one
  • VCM_REF step with C101 1 uF, R102 10 ohm; compare 87.6 degrees and 0.96 mV
  • Comparator small-signal: 5-30 mV pulses at VCM 1.65 V; delay, jitter, input noise, bias-current offset through the 1 k returns
  • Chain noise at the comparator input with the 6 pF APD connected, 20k and 5k
  • AC-coupling response to training, gaps and abrupt light changes
  • 20k/5k and bias-step switching transients: time until the slicer is valid again

Power, USB, connector

  • Supply load test, sequencing, ripple, thermal, bias startup
  • eFuse U5: OV cut-off (calculated 5.76-6.20 V), UVLO (4.06-4.32 V rising, 3.72-4.00 V falling), ILIM (0.68-0.84 A), first-plug inrush with C32 47 nF and an estimated 25 uF (about 5.3 ms, about 23 mA); check FLT and IMON
  • Fan-out U50: LVDS eye at the receptacle on both TX pairs through a 2 m certified cable at 50 Mb/s; VOD and common mode against the ECP5 +/-100 mV threshold; 3V3 current; J51 output 3 eye
  • USB auto-reset and download through the 22 ohm resistors and USBLC6 from a laptop root port; RESET/BOOT recovery; 3V3_D rise against the EN RC
  • USB-alone self-test current at dark, at 220 V with 0.5-1 mA load, and at ESP32 boot and flash peaks, against 0.19 A (0.24 A) and the 0.5 A default; CC voltages with a real source port and 2-3 laptops or hubs

G2: range-trim proof

Goal: show that the ESP32's slow trim (APD bias via DAC_HV, TIA_CTRL 20k/5k) holds the link near the Q(M) optimum using RSSI and FPGA validity, and that no analog AGC is needed because intensity changes track animal motion.

  • RSSI against photocurrent across M and 20k/5k: monotonic, repeatable, drift bounded with temperature
  • PRBS BER against photocurrent at fixed settings: confirm the about 39 dB single-setting window and the clip-region behaviour
  • Trim-loop step response: 10 dB up and down, time to valid frames, no oscillation between 20k and 5k
  • APD M against bias and temperature per unit; DAC_HV EEPROM default and HV_DIS gating verified

G3: system-behaviour proof

  • Dark start, weak light, sudden strong light, interruption and return (firmware trim plus FPGA reacquisition)
  • DAC_TH eye walk: BER against threshold offset, centre found by firmware
  • Latch, shutdown and independent-clock timeout behaviour; CAN under load; module identity
  • 6.63 MHz field injection with the module in the housing

G4: system proof

Acceptance: BER measured against known transmitted truth meets the approved target (BER <= 1e-9 is a provisional goal); redundancy works; CAD checks pass before any board order.

  • BER against known transmitted truth
  • Redundancy: skew, missing lane, conflicting frames, CRC errors, rejoin
  • Pre-order CAD checks: symbols, footprints, pin maps, ERC, DRC, stackup, independent review. Order at JLCPCB with "Min via hole size/diameter" = 0.25 mm/(0.35/0.4 mm), 6 layers, JLC06161H-3313, ENIG, impedance control; confirm the 90/100 ohm geometries in JLCPCB's calculator (ours are computed, PROPOSED). The J50 hole waiver relies on the 2026-10-09 DFM pass with that option.
  • Laptop foreign-host test: two different laptops (ideally two operating systems or vendors, USB 3 Type-C); scope the CAN pins and the LVDS pair; does the host treat the CAN load as a termination, how it handles receiver-detect and SBU; the module must stay harmless and the CAN silent; choose mitigations afterwards
  • 3 m cable check for a specific passive cable: SuperSpeed wire continuity (A2 to B11 etc.), then scope LVDS and CAN; accept 3 m only for that cable
  • CAN with 14 stubs: one split termination at the breakout, 125 kbit/s, R199 100k, stubs up to 3 m; error counters and edges
  • CAN-to-LVDS crosstalk inside the cable: CAN edges (about 2 V) against the LVDS pair (about 350 mV); BER with CAN traffic on and off
  • USB-PD charger or hub plugged into a module: stays at 5 V, tolerates a hard-reset VBUS dip; hub-powered current behaviour

10 · Open risks and UNRESOLVED items

What could still go wrong, by block

The biggest risks, in the project's own words: the APD bias supply, the 24-pin USB-C cable and foreign-host behaviour (decision 14), OPA857 behaviour at 6 pF, post-amp clipping and recovery together with comparator noise at small overdrive (decision 12), and the firmware range-control loop (M against temperature, 20k/5k scheduling). Everything below is UNRESOLVED until measured.

Detector and optics

  • Received power has never been measured. The link budget is an estimate from LED datasheets and a joint transmitter and receiver budget.
  • APD operating voltage per unit (datasheet gives VBR only; Vop is graph-read).
  • Installed detector capacitance at the actual bias, including lead and pad parasitics (6 pF is the datasheet typ).
  • Ambient is multiplied by M; the optical bandpass filter is mandatory and its real attenuation is unknown. Ambient level and maximum fade rate are unknown.
  • Emitter wavelength, intensity, drive and modulation (co-design variables).

APD bias supply

  • Ripple and noise at the cathode with the TIA running; pulse-skip versus Burst.
  • C50 capacitor dump into the TIA at a fault.
  • Temperature tracking method (NTC divider plus ESP32 DAC trim) until G1.
  • HV state if 3V3_D is absent; Q3 and EN/UVLO thresholds not rechecked; D2 purpose in the LT8365 reference circuit not stated.
  • Whether a faulted HV start can trip a USB port (hence the stepped start).

TIA, post-amp, slicer

  • OPA857 at 6 pF: datasheet gives a noise plot only; the 20k mode shows ISI in simulation.
  • Real OPA855 clip level, recovery and any limit cycle; RF/RG stability as built; idle level.
  • TLV3605 input noise and delay below 10 mV overdrive (none in the datasheet); bias current at VCM = 1.65 V; jitter added by the hysteresis network.
  • AC-coupling baseline wander with real 8b/10b traffic; slicer recovery after a CTRL or bias switch.

Connector, cable, host

  • Whether any 3 m passive full-featured cable exists; a non-compliant cable leaves only about 50 mV over the eFuse UVLO maximum.
  • Laptop and hub behaviour on the SuperSpeed pins and SBU (receiver-detect, CAN load, alternate-mode SBU drive); the USB 3.2 and PD specs were not obtained.
  • CAN with 14 stubs; CAN-to-LVDS crosstalk in the cable.
  • CC classification margin; clamp injection on the CC taps; unidirectional ESD clamps near -0.7 V on CAN.
  • Footprint checks: Wuerth part (top or mid mount), TPS259631 DDA land pattern, TPD6E05U06 USON-14, SN65LVDS104 TSSOP.
  • USB-PD charger or hub plugged into a module.

Power and controller

  • eFuse thresholds, inrush, FLT usefulness; 3V3 and 3V3_D dropout against the UVLO falling range.
  • Whether a bus-powered hub holds a module to 100 mA; the C6's USB bMaxPower descriptor.
  • USB auto-reset into download mode through the 22 ohm resistors and USBLC6; D+ pull-up backdrive into an unpowered host.
  • Firmware range-trim thresholds, dwell times and the 20k/5k schedule are first guesses. Whether to hold bias for a time on CAN loss is a Daniel decision.
  • Per-unit M calibration; absolute M needs a bench reference.
  • Self-test cannot prove the comparator or LVDS output without an FPGA or optical source.

FPGA end and system

  • Host link (FX3 first candidate; the GPIO count of 28 is a belief).
  • Breakout board not designed; the polarity constant is not yet recorded in breakout or gateware docs.
  • ECP5 input common-mode range; nextpnr DIFFRESISTOR support.
  • Burst and idle structure of the 8b/10b stream; redundant-lane skew and merge policy.
  • Master firmware, and whether the ULX3S's own ESP32 can be the CAN master.
  • BER (1e-9), reacquisition (20 ms) and LOS detection (1 ms) goals are provisional and unapproved (requirements.md item 3).

Layout and fab

  • Nothing is routed. Plane cut-out under the TIA input; exact HV island position depends on the housing.
  • Impedance geometries are computed, not JLCPCB calculator output.
  • J50 hole waiver depends on the high-precision tier and the DFM pass; no JLCPCB or LCSC listing for the part was found.
