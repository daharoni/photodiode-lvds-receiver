03

Rules while placing

  • Fast chain butted together on the top centre line: FE_APD, FE_TIA, FE_POSTAMP, FE_LPF_COUPLE, SLC_COMP, CONN_FANOUT, ESD arrays, J50.
  • APD anode to OPA857 input is the shortest trace on the board, with nothing else near it and no HV copper within a few mm on any layer.
  • HV island is one block (PWR_HV_BOOST, PWR_HV_FB, PWR_HV_DIS) with a 1.0 mm clearance ring, outer layers only; the DRC enforces the 1.0 mm clearance.
  • Only the filtered bias (R50, C50, D10 of FE_APD) runs left along the back edge to the APD cathode.
  • GND1 and GND2 stay solid under HV traces: no plane cuts.
  • Differential pairs from the fan-out to J50 stay on top over GND1, with no layer change.
  • ESD arrays within a few mm of the J50 pins; R180 termination at the fan-out input, which is centred behind J50.
  • PWR_EFUSE right beside the J50 VBUS pins with C33 at its input; CONN_CC next to the CC pins.
  • Bottom left half stays quiet: no switching parts under the front end.
  • CTRL_USB (USBLC6 + 22 R) directly under the D+/D- pins; CTRL_CAN under J50 near the RX pins.
  • ESP32-C6 module with its antenna end toward a board edge.
  • CAN termination is not on the module.
