# Schematic review: Power sheet and Connector sheet (Rev A, 2026-10-22)

Reviewer scope: `power.kicad_sch`, `connector.kicad_sch`, via `evidence/netlist.xml` (exact ref/pin to net), plus the as-built/spec docs and datasheets named below. Read-only. Nothing measured or simulated by me; all numbers below are arithmetic from the cited documents.

Datasheet sources used: LT8365 Rev. A (`docs/datasheets/lt8365.pdf`, pp.2-4, 7, 30), DC2838A REV02 schematic (`docs/datasheets/dc2838a-schematic.pdf`, rendered), TPS2596 SLVSET8A (`docs/research/downloads/usbc-power/tps2596.pdf`, pp.4-7, 22, 28), SN65LVDS104 SLLS396G (`docs/research/downloads/lvds-fanout/sn65lvds104.pdf`, pp.3, 16), MCP4728 DS22187E (`docs/datasheets/mcp4728.pdf`, Table 4-2 p.24), TPS799 SBVS056L (pin map only).

## Executive summary
1. No BLOCKER found: pin-to-net wiring of U3 (LT8365), U5 (TPS259631), U1/U4, Q1-Q3, U50, U51/U52 and J50 matches the datasheet pin maps, and the HV_DIS gate polarity gives HV OFF at power-up with the ESP32 unprogrammed or Hi-Z (VERIFIED by netlist + logic).
2. MAJOR: the 20 ohm resistor R7 is in series with D1 (stage-1 rectifier, carries the full inductor current) instead of in the pump-charge branch (V1 to D3) as in DC2838A REV02 and LT8365 p.30. It eats about 7 V of the SW-pin margin to 150 V.
3. MAJOR: with RV1/R15 DNP ("production") the bias range at 25 C is about 106-167 V, not the required 100-220 V; the 215 V figure in the docs needs the pot leg fitted. Units with VBR near 200 V cannot be biased.
4. MAJOR: 4.99 Mohm 0603 resistors (R17, R18, R20, R32) see up to 110 V each, above the usual 75 V working rating of 0603 chip resistors; and the Q1/R17/R18 "clamp" is a base-starved pass device that drops about Ic x 10 Mohm / hFE, not the "~1.2 V" in the calc doc.
5. MAJOR (single-fault): no independent hardware over-voltage limit on the HV chain; an open in the 8.2 Mohm FBX ladder drives FBX to ~0 V and the converter runs away past the 150 V SW rating.

## BLOCKER
None found.

## MAJOR

### M1. R7 (20 ohm) is in the wrong branch of the doubler
- Location: Power sheet, D1/R7/HV_V1; netlist: `D1 pin1(K)=Net-(D1-K)`, `R7 pin1=Net-(D1-K) pin2=/Power/HV_V1`, `D3 pin1(K)=HV_PUMP pin2(A)=HV_V1`, `D4 K=HV_RAW A=HV_PUMP`, `C6 SW-node to HV_PUMP`, `C7 HV_V1 to HV_RAW`, `C5 HV_V1 to GND`.
- Saw: the series resistor sits between D1 cathode and V1, so the whole boost-inductor discharge current (Ipk 0.36 A worst case in the calc doc) flows through it. D3 (pump charge diode) goes straight to V1 with no resistor.
- Reference: DC2838A REV02 schematic (rendered image): D1 goes SW to the V1 node directly; R10 (20 ohm) is in series with D3 (V1 -> R10 -> D3 -> NET1). LT8365 p.30 (the 5 V-in precedent): R8 20 ohm in series with D2 (V1 -> R8 -> D2 -> pump node), D1 direct. VERIFIED by viewing both images. `docs/calculations/apd-bias-design.md` section 3.2 itself calls it "Pump R (R8) 20 ohm ... as DC2838A R10", so this is a placement error against the author's own intent, not a deliberate change.
- Why it matters: SW peak = V1 + Vd + Ipk x 20 ohm. At V1 = 110 V and Ipk = 0.355 A that is about 118 V instead of 111 V; with the doc's 20 % ring allowance about 142 V against the 150 V abs max (LT8365 p.2), margin about 8 V instead of the 17 V claimed in section 5 of the calc doc (arithmetic, ring factor is the doc's ASSUMED value). Any current-limit event near full V1 (ILIM 1.5-2.7 A, p.3) adds up to 30-54 V and exceeds 150 V. The pump branch loses the damping resistor the reference uses.
- Fix (PROPOSED): D1 cathode directly to HV_V1; put R7 between HV_V1 and D3 anode (net HV_V1 -> R7 -> D3 pin 2). Schematic is GUI-edit only (generators frozen); netlist/PCB change is one trace.
- Confidence: HIGH that it differs from the reference; MED on the severity (ring amplitude at 5 V in is unknown, G1 SW-waveform measurement will show it).

### M2. Production HV range does not reach 220 V (pot leg DNP)
- Location: Power sheet FBX ladder; RV1 50k trim and R15 274k are DNP (schematic `dnp yes`); R13 10k + R14 442k DAC leg; R8-R11 4 x 2.05M; R12 86.6k + TH1 10k NTC.
- Saw / arithmetic (FBX 1.600 V typ, LT8365 p.3): with the pot leg open, Vout = 1.6 + 8.2 Mohm x (1.6/Rb + (1.6 - Vdac)/442k), Rb = 96.6k at 25 C. Vdac = 0 V gives 167.1 V; Vdac = 3.3 V gives 105.8 V. So authority is about 106-167 V (25 C, +/- Vfb tolerance -4.3/+4.8 V), shifted by about 0.65 V/C of tempco compensation across temperature. The calc doc and `module-controller.md` quote "215 V hardware maximum" and "DAC alone +/-30.6 V about mid", which only holds with RV1/R15 fitted. The requirement in CLAUDE.md and decision-log is 100-220 V; `apd-selection.md` says VBR spread is 150-200 V, bias for M ~ 100 about 135-190 V.
- Why it matters: a high-VBR S12023-10 (VBR near 200 V) cannot be biased to its operating point in the production build; the DAC alone leaves roughly 106-167 V. Also the low end is only about 99 V at 15 C.
- Fix (PROPOSED, not simulated): either fit R15 + a fixed divider in place of RV1 (so the coarse leg is present), or lower R14 (about 220-330 k) and re-centre Rb to keep the 135 V mid point; re-run `tools/apd_bias_design.py`. Decide the range requirement vs the VBR bins first.
- Confidence: HIGH on arithmetic (VERIFIED against LT8365 FBX value and netlist); the requirement interpretation is the open point.

### M3. High-value 0603 resistors at 110 V; clamp is base-starved, not a 1.2 V clamp
- Location: R17, R18 (Q1 base feed, 2 x 4.99M, `HV_OUT -> R17 -> mid -> R18 -> Q1 base`), R20 and R32 (HV_MON, 2 x 4.99M), all footprint R_0603.
- Saw (a, voltage rating): in current-limit or fault the Q1 base is pulled to ~1 V so R17+R18 carry the full 220 V, 110 V each; R20/R32 carry 110 V each continuously at 220 V. Typical 0603 thick-film chip resistors are rated 75 V working (Yageo RC0603 / Vishay CRCW0603 families, from memory; no MPN is on the schematic and the calc doc lists "HV 0603 resistor voltage rating NOT CHECKED"). Status: UNRESOLVED (no datasheet in repo), but 110 V over a 75 V rating is a derating violation if the typical rating holds. FBX ladder R8-R11 at 55 V each is inside it.
- Fix (PROPOSED): use three 0603 in series per leg (about 73 V each at 220 V is still too close), or 0805/1206 high-voltage parts (0805 typically 150 V, 1206 200 V) or HV thick-film series (e.g. Vishay CRHV, Susumu RG); pick an MPN and cite its rating.
- Saw (b, clamp drop): Q1 base current is supplied only through 10 Mohm from HV_OUT (R17+R18), so V(Q1 emitter) = HV_OUT - Ib x 10M - Vbe with Ib = Ic/hFE. APD current Ic drops the bias by Ic x 10 Mohm / hFE: 1 V per 10 uA at hFE 100, 2 V per 10 uA at hFE 50, and 10-20 V at 100 uA. The calc doc section 4 assumes "DC drop ... + ~1.2 V clamp" at 100 uA, which ignores this term. MMBTA42 hFE at 10-100 uA is not given in the repo (datasheet not in repo): UNRESOLVED. With the S12023 gain slope of about 7 %/V (apd-selection.md), mean photocurrent of a few uA already costs several percent of gain, unit-to-unit and over temperature (hFE tempco). It also means the Q2 current-limit knee (Vbe/R16 ~ 150 uA) is not what limits current first at low HV_OUT.
- Fix (PROPOSED): add a base-bias path with less starvation (e.g. 2 M total instead of 10 M, accepting HV/Rb fault current 110 uA, which then needs rechecking against the 200 uA Ir max), or use a Darlington/MOSFET pass device (HV depletion/enhancement FET with Q2 sense) so the base term disappears; or accept and calibrate with HV_MON at bias time. Needs a G1 sweep (calc doc section 7 item 5 already plans "inject 10-250 uA", add a measurement of the bias at the APD side, not at HV_OUT).
- Confidence: HIGH that the term exists (follows from the netlist topology); MED on magnitude (hFE unknown).

### M4. No independent hardware OV limit on the HV chain
- Location: Power sheet U3 FBX (net `/Power/FBX`), R8-R11, HV_OUT.
- Saw: LT8365 FBX is the only regulation path; the part has no output OVP (datasheet text p.7: FBX "reduces the switching frequency during start-up and fault conditions when FBX is close to 0 V", not a stop). A single open in the 8.2 Mohm series chain (four 0603, one fault) or lost FBX contact sends FBX toward 0 V; the controller keeps boosting. Failure bound: SW pin 150 V (LT8365 p.2), D1/D3/D4 150 V (DFLS1150), C5-C7 200 V, plus 2 x V1 at HV_RAW. The HV_MON path (R20/R32/R21 to ADS1115) only helps if the ESP32 runs firmware that polls it and drives HV_DIS in time, which is outside the analog-first fast path but is the only HV protection (decision 11 gating is firmware-controlled).
- Fix (PROPOSED): classic feedback Zener: a 240 V (e.g. 2 x 120 V in series) or 3 x 82 V Zener string from HV_OUT to FBX (or from V1 at about 130 V through a diode) so any overvoltage raises FBX and turns the converter off; size the leakage against the 17 uA divider current. Alternative: split Rt into two groups with the Zener across the lower half. Not simulated.
- Confidence: MED (design practice, no datasheet statement that the part lacks OVP beyond the text above).

## MINOR

### m1. D2 purpose (UNRESOLVED in docs) is a SW-node clamp in the p.30 precedent
- Location: D2 (DNP), `pin1(K)=Net-(D1-A) (SW)`, `pin2(A)=GND`. LT8365 p.30 (rendered): D4 anode GND, cathode SW, i.e. a clamp from SW to ground; DC2838A REV02 (the validated demo) omits it. The docs say "purpose not stated". VERIFIED as drawn; function (catching SW undershoot below GND from pump-cap/inductor ringing) is PROPOSED inference.
- Recommend: keep the pad, fit it for the first bench build (cheap, rated 150 V, sits on the SW node so keep the loop short) and remove it only if the SW waveform shows no undershoot. Confidence LOW-MED.

### m2. VBUS has no ESD/TVS clamp ahead of the eFuse
- Location: Connector J50 A4/A9/B4/B9 -> Power C33 4.7 uF 25 V -> U5 IN. U61 USBLC6-2SC6 pin 5 is VBUS only as a reference for the D+/D- clamps. TPS2596 IN: abs max 21 V (p.5), HBM 2 kV (p.5). Hot-plug ring on a 5 V cable with ceramic C33 can reach about 2x (10 V, arithmetic, not measured); the eFuse OVLO threshold is about 6.0 V (see checked list) so a plug-in ring may trip it (nuisance reset, no damage); an IEC contact-discharge hit on VBUS has no clamp. `usbc-power-path.md` lists a DNP TVS footprint ahead of the eFuse as an option (TPS2596 p.34 Fig. 62), but it is not on the sheet.
- Fix (PROPOSED): add a DNP/fitted SMF5.0A-class TVS at J50 VBUS. Confidence MED.

### m3. Stale/incorrect statements in the docs about the DAC leg
- `apd-bias-design.md` 3.2 says "DAC pin Hi-Z at reset -> 135.9 V". MCP4728 factory EEPROM default is VREF = internal 2.048 V, code 0x000, normal mode (DS22187E Table 4-2 p.24, VERIFIED), i.e. DAC_HV outputs 0 V at power-up, which is the HIGH-bias end: 167 V with RV1/R15 DNP, 215 V with the pot leg fitted. `module-controller.md` already documents this and the EEPROM-programming mitigation; the calc doc and the Power as-built text should be corrected. Effect today: J1 "HV FORCE ON" fitted on a fresh MCP4728 gives about 167 V at the APD (current-limited to about 150 uA by Q1/Q2). The gating default (HV off until the ESP32 drives HV_DIS low) is unaffected.

### m4. HV_DIS fails open if 3V3_D is absent
- Location: R33 100k pull-up goes to 3V3_D (not +5V); Q3 gate net `Net-(J1-Pin_1)`. If U4 or 3V3_D fails while +5V/5V_HVIN is up, the gate floats, Q3 is not guaranteed on, and EN/UVLO is held by the 200k/200k divider: HV can start with the ESP32 dead and the DAC at its default (0 V = max bias). Documented as UNRESOLVED in power.md ("a 3V3_D-independent pull-up to +5V was not added").
- Fix (PROPOSED): pull the gate up to +5V (or add a second 100k to +5V) instead of 3V3_D; DMN2056U-7 Vgs abs max is the symbol-description value ("Vgs(th) 0.4-1.0 V", abs max not rechecked), so check +/-8 V; ESP32 GPIO0 then sees 5 V through 101k (about 14 uA into its clamp, not checked). Confidence MED.

### m5. HV_MON loading of the ADS1115 and CC_SENSE back-power
- HV_MON source impedance is 99 kohm (R20+R32 || R21... = 100 kohm to GND) plus R206 10k into the ADS1115 (6 Mohm differential at +/-4.096 V, `module-controller.md`, citing ADS1115 SBAS444E p.5): about 1.7 % gain error, tens of ppm of drift from the ADC input resistor tolerance (arithmetic). Calibratable; matters only if HV_MON is used as a safety readback with tight limits.
- CC1/CC2 taps (R185/R187, R188/R189) feed ESP32 GPIO4/5 from the cable side while the ESP32 rail (3V3_D) is behind the eFuse: host Rp (or VCONN, 2.75 V after the divider) can back-power 3V3_D through the pin clamp at about 10-27 uA before/after the eFuse closes. The resulting partial rail could hover below the ESP32 POR window. UNRESOLVED, LOW confidence; check at first power-up with the eFuse tripped (hold OVLO).

## QUESTIONS / UNRESOLVED
- Q1. FBX stability: loop closed on HV_OUT through 8.2 Mohm, doubler in the loop, no simulation (apd-bias-design.md section 8 admits it). C26 (10 pF DNP) is FBX-to-GND, not across Rt like the DC2838A feed-forward C5 (OPT) position; a stray 1 pF on a 95 kohm node is a pole near 1.7 MHz (arithmetic). Plan a G1 step-response check or an LTspice run with the LT8365 model.
- Q2. L1 Wurth 74437324100: label says "1.5 A" but LT8365 ILIM can be up to 2.7 A (p.3); saturation current of this part is not in the repo (calc doc says "saturate above 2.7 A" from DS p.14). Same part as the p.1/p.30 and DC2838A circuits, so low risk; verify Isat from the Wurth datasheet.
- Q3. LVDS104 channel 3 (J51 debug) unloaded with R177 DNP. LVDS104 SLLS396G has no statement about open outputs (VERIFIED: searched the text); outputs are current-steering, so harmless by inference; consider fitting R177 for bench use or leaving EN3 = GND until needed (EN3 is tied to 3V3 now).
- Q4. SBU/AUX path: ESP32 GPIO7 -> R204 1k -> R186 1k -> J50 SBU1/SBU2 (tied) with a TPD6E05U06 channel (U52 pin 8, VRWM 5.5 V). A host that biases SBU above 3.6 V through the 2 kohm sees only the ESP32 pin clamp (about 0.7 mA at 5 V, arithmetic); no 3.3 V clamp or switch. Already UNRESOLVED in `lvds-fanout-and-foreign-host.md` item 8; recommend the clamp/switch before G4 foreign-host testing.
- Q5. USB-C sink behaviour as built (VERIFIED from netlist): R181 5.1k 1 % on A5 (CC1), R184 5.1k 1 % on B5 (CC2), each with a 100k/100k tap (effective Rd about 4.97 kohm, inside the Rd tolerance used by the docs), no VCONN, no PD, Rd always present (not dependent on the eFuse), so a laptop sees a plain UFP and turns on vSafe5V; with an e-marked cable the VCONN on the unused CC sees Rd plus the tap, as the sheet notes. Open items remain as in decision 14: bus-powered hub limit (100 mA), foreign-host Rx.Detect on the CAN pair, SS pairs carrying LVDS/CAN. Not tested.
- Q6. eFuse FLT is not routed to the ESP32 (pull-up R39 DNP, TP4 only): consequence documented by the team; I agree there is no good fix because 3V3_D is off during cut-off.
