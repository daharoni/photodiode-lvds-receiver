## 1. Decision proposed

Replace the 16-pin USB-C receptacle J60 (Controller sheet) and the 12-pin Wuerth system header J50 (Connector sheet) with ONE 24-pin full-featured USB-C receptacle per module, mated through a passive full-featured C-to-C cable. The receptacle carries VBUS/GND (5 V), D+/D- (ESP32-C6 USB Serial/JTAG, with Dp1 tied to Dp2 and Dn1 to Dn2 so either plug orientation works), SS TX1 and TX2 (the module LVDS output, duplicated through an SN65LVDS104 fan-out buffer so both pairs carry the same signal), SS RX1 and RX2 (CAN_H on both RXp pins, CAN_L on both RXn pins, bused), and SBU1 and SBU2 tied together as one AUX line. The FPGA-end "breakout" board supplies 5 V per port (one Type-C source port per module, VBUS only after Rd is seen, VCONN for the e-marked cable), wires RX1 to the ECP5 LVDS input, puts its own CAN on TX1/TX2 onto the shared bus, and routes D+/D- into a USB hub so one computer sees every module's console. The module keeps CAN plus LVDS for the FPGA and USB for the computer. Because the plug orientation is free, the module needs no orientation logic: in all four plug-flip combinations the LVDS lands on both breakout RX pairs and the breakout CAN lands on both module RX pairs (section 2).

## 2. Pinout

### 2.1 Module receptacle (Wuerth 632723300011; pin names per TC Table 3-4 p.76, VERIFIED in CONN section 1.1; Wuerth labels A5/B5 "CC"/"VCONN" (WE p.3), wired here as CC1/CC2 per the receptacle names, PROPOSED reading, CONN 4.2)

Shell: separate, GND through the existing shield RC (C193 + R202 convention from J60, PROPOSED to carry over). All VBUS pins bussed, all GND pins bussed on the PCB (TC Table 3-4 note, CONN 1.1). The Dp1/Dp2 and Dn1/Dn2 ties must be within 3.5 mm of the receptacle (TC Table 3-4 note 1 p.77, VERIFIED in CONN summary item 2); this is a layout rule.

### 2.2 Why orientation does not matter (CONN section 2.2, PROPOSED analysis computed from TC Tables 3-4 and 3-10; cable wiring VERIFIED TC Table 3-10 p.83)

The standard full-featured C-to-C cable maps plug-1 TX1 to plug-2 RX1 and TX2 to RX2 (and the reverse), p to p and n to n, crosses SBU_A/SBU_B, and carries CC on a single wire. A plug flip swaps TX1<->TX2, RX1<->RX2, SBU1<->SBU2, CC1<->CC2 at that end with polarity preserved. Module = plug 1 end, breakout = plug 2 end:

| Module end | Breakout end | Module LVDS (TX1 / TX2) lands on breakout | Breakout CAN (TX1 / TX2) lands on module | SBU (module SBU1 / SBU2) reaches breakout | CC wire |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| normal | normal | RX1 / RX2 | RX1 / RX2 | SBU2 / SBU1 | CC1 to CC1 |
| normal | flipped | RX2 / RX1 | RX2 / RX1 | SBU1 / SBU2 | CC1 to CC2 |
| flipped | normal | RX2 / RX1 | RX2 / RX1 | SBU1 / SBU2 | CC1 to CC2 |
| flipped | flipped | RX1 / RX2 | RX1 / RX2 | SBU2 / SBU1 | CC1 to CC1 |

In every row the module's two driven pairs map onto the set {RX1, RX2} at the breakout, so the breakout takes LVDS from RX1 alone (RX2 terminated 100 ohm) and always sees correct polarity. The same argument in reverse puts the breakout CAN on both module RX pairs. SBU1 tied to SBU2 at both ends makes the swap irrelevant: both cable SBU wires connect module net AUX_CAB to breakout AUX with no loop (CONN 2.2). Cable SBU is single-ended, 32 to 53 ohm, <= 5 ohm DC, 2 dB loss at 0.5 MHz, 4 dB at 1 MHz (TC Table 3-24 p.113, VERIFIED in CONN), so AUX is a slow signal, about <= 1 MHz (PROPOSED). Only CC reaches the far end; the plug's other CC position is VCONN (Ra 0.8 to 1.2 kohm, TC Table 4-29 p.246).

## 3. Decisions made in this draft

The choices below are the orchestrator's reconciliation of CONN, FAN and PWR. Where the three documents disagreed, section 7 says how it was settled.

**a. Receptacle: Wuerth 632723300011 (top pick), GCT USB4115-03-C (alternative). Daniel picks horizontal vs vertical.**
- Wuerth 632723300011: 24 pins, horizontal "Type C Hybrid 1.6 mm" (row A 12 SMD pads, row B plated through-holes, four shield posts), 5 A, 10,000 cycles, 30 mohm contact resistance max, -40 to +105 degC, WE rev 003.000 p.1 and p.3 (VERIFIED). Reason: through-hole row and shield posts give better retention for a lab module that is plugged by hand (PROPOSED).
- GCT USB4115-03-C: 24 pins, vertical SMT, H = 9.30 mm, 20,000 cycles, -25 to +85 degC, GCT4115 rev B p.1-2 (VERIFIED). Bundled KiCad footprint `Connector_USB:USB_C_Receptacle_GCT_USB4115-03-C`; pad geometry not cross-checked against GCT4115 p.2 (UNRESOLVED).
- The Wuerth part has no footprint in the bundled KiCad library or the lab library (CONN 4.2, VERIFIED by file search on this machine); a new lab-library symbol and footprint are required (section 5). Whether the Wuerth part is top- or mid-mount is not determined from the drawing (UNRESOLVED).
- GCT USB4110 and USB4085 are 16-contact USB 2.0 parts and are excluded (VERIFIED, GCT drawings, CONN 4.1). Amphenol 12401610E4#2A drawing was not obtainable (HTTP 403): UNRESOLVED, not selected.

**b. Fan-out buffer: SN65LVDS104PW.**
- TLV3605 (VOD 250/350/450 mV, VOCM 1.125/1.2/1.375 V, TLV p.7) drives the buffer input; the buffer needs +-100 mV and accepts 0 to VCC-0.8 V common mode (LVDS104 p.4-5): margin 150 mV at minimum VOD (VERIFIED arithmetic, FAN 1.3). Driving both pairs directly from the TLV3605 is rejected: 50 ohm load, about 125 mV VOD at minimum, only 25 mV over the ECP5 +-100 mV threshold (ECP5 DS Table 3.13 p.58), and the hysteresis tap R87/C87 window halves and depends on whether a cable is plugged (PROPOSED arithmetic, FAN 2).
- 100 ohm 0.1 % at the buffer input (no on-chip termination, LVDS104 p.16 Fig. 25, VERIFIED); this is the existing R180, which stops being DNP and moves from the connector to the buffer input. Outputs 1 and 2 go to TX1 and TX2. EN1, EN2 tied to 3V3; EN4 low; EN3 low by default (enables have no documented pull-up, tie each, LVDS104 p.4; VERIFIED). Option (PROPOSED): enable output 3 by a solder choice and take it to J51 as a bench LVDS debug pair.
- 3V3 budget: LVDS104 supply current 23 mA typ, 35 mA max, all four outputs loaded (LVDS104 p.5, VERIFIED); with outputs 3/4 disabled the actual draw is lower but the datasheet gives only the all-enabled and all-disabled figures (all disabled 3 mA typ, 8 mA max). Existing 3V3 estimate about 66 mA (power.md) rises by up to 35 mA to about 101 mA, inside the 200 mA TPS79901 range (TPS799 SBVS056L; FAN notes power.md did not recheck this rating: UNRESOLVED).
- Fail-safe (VERIFIED, LVDS104 p.15-16): with no TLV3605 power the buffer drives a static high on all outputs; harmless DC state for a foreign host (consequence PROPOSED).
- Outputs Hi-Z when disabled or VCC < 1.5 V, IOS +-10 mA, abs max on outputs -0.5 to 4 V (LVDS104 p.4-5, VERIFIED).
- Alternative: SN65LVDS22PW in splitter mode (about 13 mA typ / 20 mA max, but only "up to 250 Mbps" and no jitter data, LVDS22 p.1, p.6). New lab-library symbol required either way; the TSSOP-16 footprint already exists in the lab library (`AharoniLab_Package_SO:TSSOP-16_4.4x5mm_P0.65mm`) and bundled KiCad; TI drawing not compared (UNRESOLVED).
- J51 (PROPOSED): keep, redefine as an LVDS/CAN scope header: 1 GND, 2 LVDS_DBG_P (buffer 3Y), 3 LVDS_DBG_N (3Z), 4 CAN_H, 5 CAN_L, 6 AUX. RSSI, HV_MON, 3V3, 3V3_D stay on test points TP50-TP55.

**c. CAN.**
- R199 (HVD230 RS resistor) from 10 k to 100 k. With 10 k the rise time is 80/120/160 ns and the SLLA270 p.9 one-third-critical-length rule allows a stub of only about 2.7 m at the minimum edge; with 100 k the rise time is 600/800/1200 ns (HVD230 p.8) and the same rule allows at least 20 m, with t(LOOP) 535 to 990 ns still about 12 % of the 8 us bit at 125 kbit/s (VERIFIED datasheet numbers, PROPOSED arithmetic, FAN 4.2). Bench-verify.
- Termination at the breakout only (split 2 x 60.4 ohm + 4.7 nF, SLLA270 p.4 and p.8 Fig. 10); the module's R182/R183/C180 stay DNP for single-module bench use. Two bused cable pairs form about 45 ohm in parallel; acceptable at these edge rates (PROPOSED).
- Firmware keeps CAN silent until it sees bus traffic or the FPGA master, so a lone module on a laptop never transmits into the host's SS receive pair (PROPOSED, FAN 3.2 item 6). The HVD230 RS pin is a fixed resistor (R199 91k), not a GPIO, so silence means not transmitting (listen-only); standby mode is not available.
- HVD230 CANH/CANL tolerate -4 to 16 V and +-25 V transient (HVD230 p.5, VERIFIED); input resistance 20 to 50 kohm per pin (p.7) is three orders above a USB 3 receiver termination of 18 to 30 ohm (TUSB p.8, secondary), so a USB 3 host should not detect a receiver (PROPOSED; USB 3.2 spec not obtained).

**d. Power input: TPS259631DDAR eFuse replaces the TPS2121 U5 (fallback: TPS2121 with IN2 grounded).**
- Reason: with one source there is no mux to build; what is still needed is overvoltage cut-off below the 7 V abs max of TPS79901 (TPS799 p.4), soft start, a real 0.5 to 1 A current limit, and an input UVLO. TPS2121 cannot set a limit below about 1.24 A typ at RILM = 100 k and has a fixed UV of 2.5 to 2.8 V (TPS2121 p.7, p.13); TPS259631 gives all four in an 8-pin SOIC-EP, 21 V abs max on IN (TPS2596 p.1, p.5). A TVS plus polyfuse alone is rejected (PWR 1.3).
- Values (PROPOSED, from PWR 1.4; thresholds calculated with datasheet tolerances and 1 % resistors, script in PWR's scratchpad, not simulated): R35 357 k (IN to EN/UVLO), R36 43.2 k (EN/UVLO to OVLO), R37 100 k (OVLO to GND), R38 1.21 k (ILM to GND, never open), C32 47 nF X7R 10 V (dVdt), C33 4.7 uF 25 V X5R 0805 (VBUS input). OV cut-off rising 5.76 to 6.20 V; UV rising 4.06 to 4.32 V, falling 3.72 to 4.00 V; ILIM 0.68 / 0.76 / 0.84 A (TPS2596 Eq. 5 p.22, +-10.4 %); dVdt about 0.94 V/ms, 5.3 ms ramp, about 23 mA inrush into 25 uF (25 uF is an estimate, PWR 5 item 10).
- Variant TPS259631 chosen for x1 auto-retry (Device Comparison p.3). The fixed-clamp variants are rejected: a sustained 12 V input would dissipate about 1.6 W in the FET (arithmetic, PWR 1.3).
- Pin map: 1 GND, 2 dVdt, 3 EN/UVLO, 4 IN, 5 OUT, 6 FLT, 7 ILM, 8 OVLO, exposed pad GND (TPS2596 p.4, VERIFIED). FLT: optional R39 100 k pull-up to +5V and a test point; not routed to the ESP32 (unpowered during cut-off). ILM node: IMON test point, about 0.79 V/A (PWR 1.4).
- Footprint: bundled candidates `Package_SO:HSOP-8-1EP_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm_EP2.41x3.1mm` and `Package_SO:TI_SO-PowerPAD-8`; which matches the DDA land pattern (TPS2596 p.46) is UNRESOLVED, compare pad rows before layout. The lab library has no PowerPAD SOIC footprint (its `AharoniLab_Package_SO` has plain SOIC-8 only) and no TPS259631 symbol, so a library PR is needed.
- Nets removed: `+5V_CABLE`, `PWR_SRC`, the VBUS_SENSE divider (R215/R216/C205, repurposed in e), the old priority divider and OV dividers (R39-R42 as built; R39 is re-created as the FLT pull-up above), C34, and PWR_FLAG `#FLG04` on `+5V_CABLE` (moves to `VBUS`). VBUS present is implied by the MCU running; no VBUS monitor in Rev A (the ADS1115 AIN3 stays the ADC zero check, PWR 2.4).
- Fallback if Daniel wants zero new library parts for Rev A (PWR 1.2, PROPOSED): keep TPS2121 with IN1 = VBUS and IN2, OV2, CP2, PR1, ST tied to GND (the datasheet's truth table row IN2 <= UV selects IN1, Table 9-3 p.18, VERIFIED; no datasheet sentence on a grounded IN2 itself, UNRESOLVED); delete R35-R37, R41, R42, C34; keep R38, C32, C33, R39, R40. Cost: current limit cannot go below about 1.24 A, fixed UV 2.5 to 2.8 V, 10 of 12 pins wasted, 300 to 400 uA quiescent.
- Margin check: a Type-C source is 4.75 to 5.5 V; a compliant 3 A cable drops at most 75 mV loop at 0.3 A (TC 4.4.1 p.151, arithmetic), so the module sees at least about 4.66 V against UV rising 4.06 to 4.32 V (PWR 3). A non-compliant 28 AWG 3 m cable would drop about 0.38 V at 0.3 A (computed, CONN 3.4), which takes 4.75 V to about 4.37 V, only about 50 mV above the 4.32 V maximum rising threshold: UNRESOLVED, the reason for requiring a compliant cable (h).

**e. Source and orientation sensing.**
- CC1 and CC2 go to ESP32-C6 IO4 (pad 9, ADC1_CH4) and IO5 (pad 10, ADC1_CH5), the pins freed by VBUS_SENSE and PWR_SRC; every other ADC1 pin IO0 to IO6 is already used (PWR 2.4).
- Network (PROPOSED, orchestrator choice; PWR proposed 47 k + 100 nF instead): per CC pin, 100 k in series to the pin, 100 k from the pin to GND, 100 nF from the pin to GND (R215/R216/C205 reused for CC1; CC2 gets the next free R/C in the Controller ranges). The 5.1 k Rd (R200/R201, upgraded to 1 %, needed to detect the advertisement, TC2.0 Table 4-25 p.236) stays at the receptacle. Reason for 100 k/100 k over 47 k: the divider keeps the pin under 3.3 V even if the e-marked cable puts its 5.5 V VCONN (vVCONNValid 3.0 to 5.5 V, TC R2.5 p.155; CONN summary item 4) on the unused CC pin: 5.5 V x 100/200 = 2.75 V at the pin, versus about 5.5 V behind 47 k, which would push about 30 uA into the pin clamp (PWR 2.4). PWR 2.1 (TC2.0 p.36, p.139) states VCONN is isolated from the far end of the cable so the unused pin sees only the plug's Ra; CONN does not rely on that (cable wire 18 connection not verified, UNRESOLVED). The design tolerates either reading.
- Numbers (arithmetic): compliant source CC maximum 2.04 V (PWR 2.1, TC2.0 Table 4-36 p.241) becomes 1.02 V at the pin; normal class voltages 0.42 / 0.94 / 1.69 V (56 k / 22 k / 10 k) become 0.21 / 0.47 / 0.85 V; the thresholds 0.66 V and 1.23 V become 0.33 V and 0.615 V. Note the Rd loading error: the 200 k tap in parallel with 5.1 k lowers effective Rd by about 2.5 %, which lowers every class voltage by about 2.5 % (arithmetic; fold into the thresholds or use a larger series resistor).
- ADC attenuation: the only range the PWR doc read is ATTEN2, 0 to 1.9 V with total error +-23 mV after calibration (ESP32-C6 datasheet v1.5 Table 5-6 p.66, as cited in PWR 2.4). ATTEN2 covers the 1.02 V maximum at the pin. Referred to the CC pin the +-23 mV error is +-46 mV, against class-edge margins of about 40 to 50 mV (PWR 2.4, before the divider halving them to 20 to 25 mV at the pin): the classification is therefore thin; require three consecutive agreeing reads across more than tRpValueChange (10 to 20 ms, TC2.0 Table 4-31 p.239-240). Whether a lower-attenuation setting is available and its accuracy are not read: UNRESOLVED (check the TRM ADC chapter / datasheet Table 5-6 before layout; if a finer range exists the divider can be dropped to a 2:1 with less error loss).
- IO4/IO5 are MTMS/MTDI strapping pins that only select SDIO sampling edges (MINI-1 datasheet Table 4-4 p.14); SDIO unused, PROPOSED harmless. The 100 k series plus 100 k shunt make a 50 k Thevenin source with a 5 ms time constant with the 100 nF.
- The Rp class is a hint only (logged in telemetry). Definitive indicators: LINK_ALL (0x1F0, 20 Hz) from the FPGA master on CAN means "system"; a USB_SERIAL_JTAG SOF interrupt (TRM v1.2 section 32.5 p.1035) means "laptop host present". Rp class and orientation never gate HV (HV_DIS discipline unchanged).
- Breakout advertises 1.5 A (22 k class, 1.7 A limit) via one TPS25810 per port (SLVSCR1C): VBUS applied only after Rd is attached, VCONN for the e-marked cable, FAULT and POL outputs, 3 x 4 mm WQFN-20 (TPS25810 p.1, Table 3 p.14, VERIFIED per PWR 3). A 3 A advertisement would force a 3.4 A port limit on a shared 5 V brick (PWR 2.2). VBUS gated on Rd also protects a laptop plugged into the breakout by mistake (TC 4.4.2 p.152, CONN 5.2; behaviour with a real laptop not tested, UNRESOLVED). 14 modules at 0.24 A are 3.36 A; one port at its 1.7 A fault plus 13 normal is 4.8 A, so a 5 V brick of at least 6 A (PWR 3, estimates; brick part not chosen).

**f. AUX: SBU1 and SBU2 tied as one line.**
- Net AUX_CAB goes through R186 1 k to the ESP32 IO7 (existing AUX), with ESD (g). AUX2 (IO21, the former PGOOD_VGA input, and R187) is dropped; IO21 becomes a no_connect, available later (for example as an LVDS-enable override, UNRESOLVED, not proposed here).
- The Type-C spec says a port that implements neither USB4 nor an Alternate Mode shall leave SBU open or weakly pulled down (>= 950 kohm) (TC R2.5 section 4.3 p.150, Table 4-31 p.246; TC2.0 p.139, Table 4-28 p.236, VERIFIED). Our AUX is a driven line. This is noted and accepted because neither end is a USB-compliant alternate-mode port (PROPOSED). Residual hazard: a laptop plugged in directly might drive SBU for DisplayPort alt mode, which needs USB PD discovery that the module never answers (not verified, UNRESOLVED); the 1 k series resistor and clamp limit the exposure of the 3.3 V-only ESP32 GPIO (SBU voltage limits not in hand: UNRESOLVED). Firmware holds the AUX driver high-Z until CAN LINK_ALL or the host class is known (PROPOSED).
- SBU presence signalling for source detection is rejected (PWR 2.3).

**g. ESD.**
- USBLC6-2SC6 stays on D+/D- (full speed; capacitance not an issue). Its VBUS pin clamp behaviour is not verified (existing UNRESOLVED in the spec); VBUS surge protection is the eFuse (21 V abs max), not the USBLC6.
- Two TI TPD6E05U06RVZ cover CC1, CC2, the tied SBU net and the eight SS pins (11 channels, one spare): 0.47 pF typ, VRWM 5.5 V, VBR 6.5 to 8.5 V, leakage 10 nA max (TPDxE05U06 SLVSBO7O Table 5.4 p.7, VERIFIED in CONN 4.4). The 5.5 V working voltage covers VCONN on CC. Suggested split (PROPOSED): U51 = LVDS_OUT1_P/N, LVDS_OUT2_P/N, AUX_CAB, CC1; U52 = CAN_H and CAN_L at each of the two RX pairs (four pins), CC2, one spare. Place within a few mm of the receptacle (datasheet note on D pins, p.5).
- Excluded: TI ESD122 (VRWM +-3.6 V, ESD122 SLVSDP5A Table 6.6 p.5) and Nexperia PUSB3FR4 (VI max 3.3 V, 12 July 2018 Tables 4-5 p.3): both clamp or conduct below the 5 V levels on CC, SBU and the CAN pins (CONN 4.4).
- Package check (done for this draft): TPD6E05U06 is RVZ, 14-pin USON, 1 x 2.5 mm, 0.5 mm pitch (SLVSBO7O p.5 pin table, mechanical page), six I/O pins D1+/D1-/D2+/D2-/D3+/D3- on pins 14, 13, 12, 11, 9, 8, GND on pins 5 and 10, pins 1-4, 6, 7 NC (VERIFIED, ti-tpdxe05u06.pdf). A bundled KiCad footprint exists: `Package_SON:Texas_R-PUSON-N14`, whose description cites `tpd6e05u06.pdf#page=28` and has 14 pads (checked on this machine, KiCad 10.0.5; pad geometry not compared with the TI drawing: UNRESOLVED). The lab library has no SON footprint category (`footprints/` listing checked), so a footprint PR (copy of this one) and a symbol are needed.
- Open: unidirectional clamps conduct near -0.7 V; with up to 0.4 V of cable GND drop between a module and a distant breakout, check CAN common-mode excursions against that clamp (UNRESOLVED, CONN 4.4). Clamp injection current of the ESP32 pin on the CC taps is not specified (UNRESOLVED, PWR 5 item 6).

**h. Cable.**
- Specification: "passive, full-featured (SuperSpeed wires present), USB 3.2 Gen 1, 3 A, electronically marked, <= 2 m certified". Basis: TC Table 3-1 p.44 (informative) lists full-featured passive lengths of 2 m (Gen 1) and 1 m (Gen 2), and 4 m only for USB 2.0 cables with no SS wires; all full-featured cables must be e-marked (TC 3.4.1.1 p.83); a 3 A cable has <= 250 mV GND and <= 500 mV VBUS drop at rated current (TC 4.4.1 p.151) (all VERIFIED, CONN/PWR).
- 3 m only after a bench check of a specific passive cable. Candidate: Eaton Tripp Lite U420-010 (3.05 m, "USB 3.2 Gen 1, 5 Gbps, 3 A" per reseller listing; passive vs active not stated, UNRESOLVED, market check is search snippets, CONN 3.6). A 3 m cable is not certifiable under TC Table 3-1 for passive full-featured, so a 3 m cable is likely active (redriver; avoid, a redriver is not expected to pass either 125 kbit/s CAN or continuous 50 Mb/s LVDS; PROPOSED) or USB 2.0 only (no SS wires; avoid). Bench check: DC continuity of the SS wires through the cable (A2 at plug 1 to B11 at plug 2 and so on: a few ohms for passive full-featured, open for USB 2.0-only and most active cables, CONN 3.5), then scope the LVDS and CAN through it.
- For our signals loss is not the issue (about -1.1 dB over 3 m at 25 MHz by sqrt(f) extrapolation from TC Table 3-21, an estimate below the table's range; one 90-versus-100-ohm echo of at most about 19 mV against 147 mV margin, FAN 4.1, PROPOSED, nothing measured).
- Locking is a mechanical add-on: neither receptacle has screw holes (CONN 3.6). Screw-lock parts exist from Newnex and others but the 3 m ones are listed as Gen 2x2 and probably active (UNRESOLVED).

**i. Firmware (list of edits needed, none made).**
- Replace `PWR_SRC` and `VBUS_SENSE` with `cc_class` (`default`, `1.5A`, `3.0A`, `none`), `cc_orient` (`cc1`, `cc2`, `none`) and `usb_host` (SOF seen), plus the existing LINK_ALL listen. ## 7. Reconciliation notes (inconsistencies between the three research documents)

- CC tap values: PWR proposed 47 k series + 100 nF with ATTEN2; this draft uses 100 k/100 k + 100 nF per the orchestrator, to keep 5.5 V VCONN below 3.3 V at the pin. Cost: halved CC voltages, thinner classification margin (3e).
- VCONN on the unused CC pin: CONN says a powered e-marked cable's VCONN may appear there (up to 5.5 V); PWR says (TC2.0) it is isolated from the far end so only Ra appears. Treated as unresolved and designed for the worse case.
- Breakout Rp: CONN proposed 56 k default advertisement; PWR proposed 1.5 A (22 k) through TPS25810. This draft follows PWR (1.5 A); CONN's 56 k tolerance arithmetic does not apply.
- Cable lane mapping: FAN listed TX1/TX2 to RX1/RX2 crossing as unverified; CONN verified it against TC Table 3-10 p.83. Settled by CONN.
- SBU and `cc_orient`: PWR said AUX needs the CC orientation; CONN showed that tying SBU1 to SBU2 at both ends removes the dependence. Settled by tying (3f), so the `cc_orient` requirement for AUX is dropped; `cc_orient` stays as telemetry only.
- Specification revisions: PWR cites USB Type-C R2.0 (Aug 2019) page numbers; CONN cites R2.5 (March 2026). Limits quoted here are identical where both were checked; page numbers are labelled TC (R2.5) or TC2.0.
- Reference designators: PWR text says R39-R42 are deleted but its resistor table re-uses R39 as the FLT pull-up; this draft re-creates R39 as an optional FLT pull-up.
- ESD placement: PWR assumed no new ESD parts on CC; CONN requires the TPD6E05U06 pair (CC, SBU, 8 SS pins). The 100 k series resistor (3e) is the ESP32's first-line protection; the TVS handles the connector side.
- LVDS104 current: FAN quotes the all-four-outputs figure (23 mA typ, 35 mA max); with outputs 3 and 4 disabled the real current is below that but is not given by the datasheet, so 35 mA is used as the bound.
- The CONN doc's typo `USB_C_Receptacle_GCT_USB4115-03_C` in its Summary was corrected to `Connector_USB:USB_C_Receptacle_GCT_USB4115-03-C`.
