                                                                                   Si APD

                                                                                S14645 series


                                              High speed, compact Si APD for LiDAR (900 nm band)
                                              featuring low-bias operation

   The S14645 series is a compact, surface mount type Si APD that achieves high sensitivity in the 800 nm band. This is suitable
   for laser monitoring of optical rangefinders widely used from consumer electronics to industrial use.


   Features                                                                 Applications

  Small package: 3.1 × 1.8 × 1.0t mm                                       Optical rangefinders
  Peak sensitivity wavelength: 840 nm (M=100)
  Low bias operation: Breakdown voltage=195 V max.
  High-speed response: Cutoff frequency=600 MHz typ.
   (λ=900 nm, M=100)
  Reduction of breakdown voltage variation: 175 ± 20 V
