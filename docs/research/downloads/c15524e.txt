                                                                                             MPPC® modules

                                                                                                            C15524 series


                                                              Optical measurement modules for low-level light
                                                              detection, flexible cable type, analog output

    The C15524 series are optical measurement modules capable of detecting low-level light using its built-in MPPC that features
    wide dynamic range. These modules consist of an MPPC, an amplifier, a high-voltage power supply circuit, and a temperature
    compensation circuit. The photosensitive area is available in two sizes: 1.3 × 1.3 mm and 3 × 3 mm. The signal output is
    analog. The modules operate just by connecting them to an external power supply (±5 V).

   Analog output
