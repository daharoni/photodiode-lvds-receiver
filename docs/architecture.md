# Architecture

Labels: VERIFIED (checked against a cited datasheet/measurement), PROPOSED (design intent, not yet proven), UNRESOLVED (open). Nothing here is measured; ngspice simulations with TI models (docs/simulation/README.md, label SIMULATED, not VERIFIED) cover OPA855 stability, OPA857 at 6 pF and the VCM_REF buffer; hence few VERIFIED labels. The architecture is analog-first by user decision.

The three biggest open risks are the **APD bias supply**, the **fast high/low detector** and **OPA857 behavior at 6 pF source capacitance**. The detector is a silicon APD (decision-log entry 8, PROPOSED): a bare PIN was 18-23 dB optical short at 35 cm lateral and APD gain closes the link with a modest NIR emitter. The link budget is a joint transmitter/receiver budget: the headstage emitter (wavelength, radiant intensity, drive, modulation) is redesigned to match this receiver, so neither side is fixed.

## Rails

| Rail | Source | Use | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5V in | `+5V_CABLE` (system cable, priority) or USB-C `VBUS` (fallback), merged by the TPS2121 mux U5 into the internal `+5V` rail (decision 13); up to 5.25 V | All rails | PROPOSED |
| 3V3 | TPS79901 LDO | OPA857, comparator, logic | PROPOSED |
| 3V3_D | TPS7A2033 LDO from 5 V | ESP32-C6, CAN transceiver, I2C DAC/ADC digital side | PROPOSED |
| APD_BIAS (HV) 100-220 V | Programmable, temperature-compensated, low-noise APD bias supply (IC/module TBD; options pending in docs/research/apd-bias-supply.md), RC-filtered | APD cathode via RC filter (e.g. 10 kohm + 10 nF, >= 250 V C0G, values PROPOSED) and series current limit. Needs 200 V creepage/clearance and keep-out from TIA_IN. Replaces the MAX15032 6.5-15 V block | PROPOSED (supply UNRESOLVED) |

Rev A has three low-voltage rails: analog 3V3 (TPS79901), digital 3V3_D (TPS7A2033) and the HV bias. The +/-3.7 V and 5V_A rails were removed with decision 12.

**USB standalone (decision 13, PROPOSED, nothing measured).** A module can be powered, programmed and self-tested from a laptop USB-C port alone, with no FPGA and no system cable (Daniel, 2026-10-09). U5 (TPS2121, TI SLVSEA3F) selects the cable when healthy and USB otherwise; with both plugged in the cable powers the module and USB carries data only, and reverse-current blocking keeps either source from being back-driven (SLVSEA3F p.7, p.15; to be measured in G1). Worst-case draw is about 0.19 A (0.24 A with margin) against the 0.5 A USB default, so the radio stays off. Programming uses the native USB Serial/JTAG (host RTS/DTR auto-reset, TRM v1.2 p.1030) with RESET/BOOT switches for recovery and 22 ohm D+/D- series resistors. Two sense lines tell firmware the power state: `VBUS_SENSE` (divider, IO4) and `PWR_SRC` (mux ST, IO5). HV rules are unchanged (HV_DIS default off; in USB-alone mode firmware starts HV from the low-bias end in steps). Hierarchical nets added: `+5V_CABLE` (Connector to Power), `VBUS` (Controller to Power), `PWR_SRC` (Power to Controller). Self-test cannot prove the comparator or LVDS output without an FPGA or optical source (UNRESOLVED). Detail: docs/schematic/rev-a-sheet-specs.md, docs/research/usb-standalone-power.md.

## Signal chain

1. **APD (APD_BIAS, TIA_IN).** Primary Hamamatsu S12023-10 (phi 1.0 mm, 6 pF, VBR 150 V typ / 200 V max, operating about 120-190 V for M = 50-100, dVBR/dT about 0.65 V/C, TO-18 with window; datasheet figures per docs/research/apd-selection.md). Fallback S12023-05 (phi 0.5 mm, 2 pF). Cathode to APD_BIAS through the RC filter and a series current limit, anode to TIA_IN (same polarity as before); reverse bias is about cathode voltage minus 1.83 V. Slow AGC absorbs slow gain drift, but the bias needs coarse temperature compensation (thermistor network or a future SAMD51 DAC). Ambient light is multiplied by M too, so the optical bandpass filter is mandatory and the OPA857 20k linear range (60 uA input) is the saturation bound. The HV node needs 200 V creepage/clearance and keep-out from the TIA input. FDS010 (TO-5, 6 pF) is kept only as a bench fallback footprint. Label: PROPOSED (part and bias design not final; operating voltage per unit UNRESOLVED).
2. **OPA857 TIA (TIA_OUT, TIA_REF).** A transimpedance stage first because the diode is a current source with large capacitance; converting to voltage at the source keeps noise and bandwidth under control before any variable gain. Differential output with OUTN about 1.83 V reference. SIMULATED (ngspice 47, TI OPA857 v1.0 model): stable at 6 pF in both modes, but 20k mode shows 13.8 % overshoot and 12.6 ns to settle to 2 % of the 20 ns UI (ISI risk), while 5k is clean, so 5k is preferred when the signal allows; model fidelity at 6 pF is not validated. Label: PROPOSED (behavior at 6 pF UNRESOLVED until measured, G1; datasheet gives noise plot only).
3. **AC coupling.** OPA857 OUT is never DC-wired onward: OUTN sits at 1.83 V and a DC-coupled gain stage would multiply ambient photocurrent by the gain. AC coupling removes ambient/DC photocurrent and offset at the cost of baseline wander with long runs; 8b/10b bounds run length and DC balance, which is why it is acceptable (corner about 10 us or slower). 6.63 MHz field pickup is in-band and is handled by shielding, not filtering. Label: PROPOSED.
4. **OPA855 fixed-gain post-amplifier (PA_OUT), decision 12.** Gain x7 (the OPA855 minimum stable gain), single 3.3 V supply, AC-coupled in and out. Purpose: lift the operating-point signal (15-25 mVpp at the TIA with the APD near its optimum gain) to 105-175 mVpp so the comparator's +/-5 mV offset and unspecified input noise stop mattering. Overload: output clips at about 9 uA pp of photocurrent at 20k (36 uA at 5k), recovers in 3 ns (SBOS622D), and because the clip is symmetric about the AC baseline the comparator keeps slicing up to the TIA's own 60 uA saturation. A 60-80 MHz low-pass follows. Label: PROPOSED (clip timing shift and chain noise at 6 pF are G1 measurements; docs/research/post-amp-slicer.md).
5. **Baseline slicer (VCM_REF, SDET_P/SDET_N).** The comparator inputs are AC-coupled with matched returns (<= 1 k, TLV3605 bias current up to 5 uA) to a buffered 1.65 V reference; with DC-balanced 8b/10b the eye centre is that baseline, so no threshold tracking is needed. The ESP32 can shift the threshold by a few tens of mV through DAC_TH into a summing network (offset trim, eye walking). Label: PROPOSED.
6. **Range control (firmware trim).** Received intensity changes only as fast as the animal moves, so a tens-of-ms firmware loop is fast enough (Daniel, 2026-10-08). The ESP32 holds the APD near its optimum gain (Q(M) peaks at M 35-60 for the S12023-10, link budget) via DAC_HV and switches the OPA857 between 20k and 5k on TIA_CTRL, using an RSSI peak detector on the TIA output (crude, telemetry only) read by the ADS1115 and the FPGA's 8b/10b validity. One fixed setting covers about 39 dB of signal; firmware adds about 32 dB. With the ESP32 unprogrammed the module runs at the resistor-set bias and 20k. Label: PROPOSED.
7. **Signal presence.** The FPGA's decoder validity is the LOS judge; RSSI is advisory. There is no analog LOS and nothing to freeze (the integrator is gone). Label: PROPOSED.
8. **Removed with decision 12.** VCA821 VGA, LM27762 +/-3.7 V rails, 5V_A, the fast high/low capture coupon, VMID/VSPAN, the AGC integrator, TMUX1112 freeze, TLV3201 LOS, HOLD_N. History in decision-log entries 10 and 12 and in git.
10. **ECP5 FPGA (ULX3S first).** The FPGA does clock recovery, 8b/10b decode, framing and redundant-lane merge, because no clock is sent: a digital CDR is flexible, lets the rate change, and avoids a PLL on the module. Its watchdog uses an independent local clock and the CDR and framing are reacquired after release; invalid-code counts are not a BER measurement. ULX3S true pairs: gp/gn[8..13] (bank 7, VCCIO jumper-selectable 2.5/3.3 V), gp/gn[14..21] (banks 2/3, 3.3 V); banks 0/1 (gp/gn[0..7], [22..27]) have no differential inputs. ECP5 differential inputs are powered from VCCAUX, independent of VCCIO, and on-die DIFFRESISTOR=100 is available on the left/right banks (FPGA-TN-02032 v1.4 s.4.3, s.4.10, s.4.11.8; ULX3S v3.1.7 schematic p.2-3): VERIFIED, see docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md. No external termination or LVDS receiver IC on the receiver board; a DNP 100 ohm pad at the connector is for bench scoping only. Open: ECP5 input common-mode range vs the TLV3605 1.2 V output common mode (family datasheet not retrieved), DIFFRESISTOR support in nextpnr. Label: PROPOSED.
11. **Module controller (ESP32-C6-MINI-1, decision 11).** Intended use includes continuous telemetry over CAN (RSSI, TIA gain, bias set point and HV_MON, temperature, identity, firmware version, HV state, CAN error counters) joined on the FPGA side with per-lane 8b/10b error counts for a per-module dashboard; design the CAN message set for that (Daniel, 2026-10-08). The module's single point of contact with the FPGA side over CAN (CAN_H/CAN_L in the LVDS cable, 3.3 V transceiver; master on the ULX3S side). Locally it drives an MCP4728 12-bit DAC (DAC_GAIN, DAC_TH, DAC_HV) and reads an ADS1115 ADC (HV_MON, VSPAN, VG, VMID) over I2C, and owns SHDN_N, LE_HYS, HV_EN, LOS and PGOOD on GPIO. It reports a unique module ID. It is not in the signal path and is unnecessary for the analog loop to run; HOLD_N remains a direct FPGA wire. Radio unused. Own 3V3_D LDO rail. Native USB (USB-C on the module) for programming and standalone self-test, VBUS_SENSE on IO4 and PWR_SRC on IO5 (decision 13). Label: PROPOSED (parts and pin map in docs/research/module-controller.md).

## States

| State | Entry | Behavior | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| POWER-UP | Rails up; HV off (HV_DIS high) | ESP32 verifies DAC EEPROM, enables HV at the resistor-set bias, TIA at 20k; comparator slices whatever arrives | ESP32 ready |
| SEARCH | No valid frames | Firmware steps M and 20k/5k from RSSI; FPGA searches for valid code | Valid frames |
| TRACK | Valid frames | Firmware trims M toward the Q(M) optimum slowly from RSSI and FPGA error counts | Loss of validity |
| LOS | FPGA timeout on an independent clock | Firmware returns to SEARCH scheduling; comparator state is "invalid" | Valid frames |

Dwell times and the RSSI thresholds are UNRESOLVED until G3. Provisional goals: reacquisition <= 50 ms (not approved).
