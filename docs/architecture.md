# Architecture

Labels: VERIFIED (checked against a cited datasheet/measurement), PROPOSED (design intent, not yet proven), UNRESOLVED (open). Nothing here is measured; ngspice simulations with TI models (docs/simulation/README.md, label SIMULATED, not VERIFIED) cover OPA855 stability, OPA857 at 6 pF and the VCM_REF buffer; hence few VERIFIED labels. The architecture is analog-first by user decision.

The biggest open risks are the **APD bias supply**, the **24-pin USB-C cable and foreign-host behaviour** (decision 14), **OPA857 behavior at 6 pF source capacitance**, **post-amp clipping/recovery and comparator noise at small overdrive** (decision 12) and the **firmware range-control loop**. The detector is a silicon APD (decision-log entry 8, PROPOSED): a bare PIN was 18-23 dB optical short at 35 cm lateral and APD gain closes the link with a modest NIR emitter. The link budget is a joint transmitter/receiver budget: the headstage emitter (wavelength, radiant intensity, drive, modulation) is redesigned to match this receiver, so neither side is fixed.

## Rails

| Rail | Source | Use | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5V in | USB-C `VBUS` on the module's single 24-pin receptacle, through the TPS259631DDAR eFuse U5 (OV cut-off 5.76-6.20 V, UVLO rising 4.06-4.32 V, ILIM 0.76 A typ, calculated) into the internal `+5V` rail (decision 14); up to 5.25 V | All rails | PROPOSED |
| 3V3 | TPS79901 LDO | OPA857, comparator, logic | PROPOSED |
| 3V3_D | TPS7A2033 LDO from 5 V | ESP32-C6, CAN transceiver, I2C DAC/ADC digital side | PROPOSED |
| APD_BIAS (HV) 100-220 V | On-board LT8365 boost plus charge-pump doubler (decision 9; design in docs/calculations/apd-bias-design.md, options in docs/research/apd-bias-supply.md), NTC-tracking feedback divider with the DAC_HV trim, Q1/Q2 clamp, two-stage RC filter | APD cathode via RC filter (e.g. 10 kohm + 10 nF, >= 250 V C0G, values PROPOSED) and series current limit. Needs 200 V creepage/clearance and keep-out from TIA_IN. Replaces the MAX15032 6.5-15 V block | PROPOSED (not measured; G1) |

Rev A has three low-voltage rails: analog 3V3 (TPS79901), digital 3V3_D (TPS7A2033) and the HV bias. The +/-3.7 V and 5V_A rails were removed with decision 12.

**One USB-C connector and USB standalone (decisions 13 and 14, PROPOSED, nothing measured).** Each module has ONE 24-pin full-featured USB-C receptacle (Wuerth 632723300011, horizontal; decision 14, 2026-10-09) mated through a passive full-featured C-to-C cable (rule: <= 2 m certified, 3 m only after a bench check of the specific cable). It carries 5 V VBUS/GND, D+/D- (ESP32-C6 USB Serial/JTAG, Dp1/Dp2 and Dn1/Dn2 tied, so either plug orientation works), the module LVDS on both SS TX pairs, CAN_P/CAN_N (CANH/CANL; renamed from CAN_H/CAN_L 2026-10-16 so the KiCad diff-pair router pairs them) bused on both SS RX pairs, and SBU1/SBU2 tied as the AUX line. An SN65LVDS104PW fan-out buffer U50 (100 ohm termination at its input, TLV3605 margin 150 mV at minimum VOD, SN65LVDS104 SLLS396G p.4-5) drives both TX pairs with the same polarity; output 3 goes to the J51 scope header, output 4 is disabled. Orientation therefore costs no ECP5 pairs: in all four flip combinations the LVDS lands on the breakout's RX1/RX2 and the breakout CAN lands on both module RX pairs. VBUS enters the TPS259631 eFuse (overvoltage cut-off below the 7 V abs max of the 3V3 LDO, UVLO, soft start, 0.76 A limit; TI SLVSET8A), which replaced decision 13's TPS2121 two-source mux. The system picture is CAN + LVDS between the FPGA and the modules, with USB only as a line to a computer; the module stays programmable and self-testable from a laptop over USB alone (decision 13 requirement, kept; native USB Serial/JTAG with RESET/BOOT switches and 22 ohm D+/D- series resistors). CC1/CC2 are read on IO4/IO5 (ADC) to log the source class and orientation; LINK_ALL from the FPGA master decides bus mode, and USB SOF decides "laptop host present". HV rules unchanged (HV_DIS default off; stepped start in USB-alone mode). Hierarchical nets: `VBUS` (Connector to Power and Controller), `USB_DP`/`USB_DN`, `CC1_SENSE`/`CC2_SENSE`, `AUX`; `+5V_CABLE`, `PWR_SRC` and `AUX2` are removed. Self-test cannot prove the comparator or LVDS output without an FPGA or optical source (UNRESOLVED). Detail: docs/schematic/rev-a-sheet-specs.md revision 4, docs/research/decision-14-draft-usbc-single-connector.md.

**Breakout board concept (FPGA end, PROPOSED, not designed).** One Type-C source port per module: 5 V per port applied only after Rd is seen, VCONN for the e-marked cable, an advertised 1.5 A, RX1 wired to the ECP5 LVDS input (RX2 terminated), the breakout's own CAN on TX1/TX2 onto a shared bus with the split termination, and D+/D- into a USB hub so one computer sees every module console. The ULX3S cannot be a USB host; the FPGA board connects by LVDS and CAN only.

**Host link (UNRESOLVED, separate decision, draft open item 8).** How the ULX3S reaches a computer is undecided. Preferred candidate to evaluate first (Daniel, 2026-10-09, PROPOSED): the Cypress/Infineon FX3 (CYUSB3014 family, a USB 3.0 SuperSpeed device controller with a parallel GPIF II interface, as used on the Miniscope DAQ, which the lab knows well) on the ULX3S GPIO header; being SuperSpeed it should cover the merged stream and all 14 raw lanes (no FX3 datasheet figures were read for this note). UNRESOLVED: the ULX3S GPIO pin count left after the 14 LVDS pairs is believed to be 28 pins, which would fit a 16-bit GPIF bus but not 32-bit; check against the ULX3S schematic. Other options recorded in the draft: USB 2.0 high-speed FIFO bridge into the breakout hub, USB 3.0 FIFO bridge, Ethernet PHY with a soft MAC, a single-board computer at the rig.

## Signal chain

1. **APD (APD_BIAS, TIA_IN).** Primary Hamamatsu S12023-10 (phi 1.0 mm, 6 pF, VBR 150 V typ / 200 V max, operating about 120-190 V for M = 50-100, dVBR/dT about 0.65 V/C, TO-18 with window; datasheet figures per docs/research/apd-selection.md). Fallback S12023-05 (phi 0.5 mm, 2 pF). Cathode to APD_BIAS through the RC filter and a series current limit, anode to TIA_IN (same polarity as before); reverse bias is about cathode voltage minus 1.83 V. The NTC network in the LT8365 feedback divider tracks VBR coarsely and the ESP32 trims M slowly through DAC_HV (decisions 9, 11, 12); there is no analog AGC. Ambient light is multiplied by M too, so the optical bandpass filter is mandatory and the OPA857 20k linear range (60 uA input) is the saturation bound. The HV node needs 200 V creepage/clearance and keep-out from the TIA input. The FDS010 bench-fallback footprint D11 was dropped from Rev A on 2026-10-09 (Daniel); the FDS010 remains a bench-only reference in requirements.md. Label: PROPOSED (part and bias design not final; operating voltage per unit UNRESOLVED).
2. **OPA857 TIA (TIA_OUT, TIA_REF).** A transimpedance stage first because the diode is a current source with large capacitance; converting to voltage at the source keeps noise and bandwidth under control before any variable gain. Differential output with OUTN about 1.83 V reference. SIMULATED (ngspice 47, TI OPA857 v1.0 model): stable at 6 pF in both modes, but 20k mode shows 13.8 % overshoot and 12.6 ns to settle to 2 % of the 20 ns UI (ISI risk), while 5k is clean, so 5k is preferred when the signal allows; model fidelity at 6 pF is not validated. Label: PROPOSED (behavior at 6 pF UNRESOLVED until measured, G1; datasheet gives noise plot only).
3. **AC coupling.** OPA857 OUT is never DC-wired onward: OUTN sits at 1.83 V and a DC-coupled gain stage would multiply ambient photocurrent by the gain. AC coupling removes ambient/DC photocurrent and offset at the cost of baseline wander with long runs; 8b/10b bounds run length and DC balance, which is why it is acceptable (corner about 10 us or slower). 6.63 MHz field pickup is in-band and is handled by shielding, not filtering. Label: PROPOSED.
4. **OPA855 fixed-gain post-amplifier (PA_OUT), decision 12.** Gain x7 (the OPA855 minimum stable gain), single 3.3 V supply, AC-coupled in and out. Purpose: lift the operating-point signal (15-25 mVpp at the TIA with the APD near its optimum gain) to 105-175 mVpp so the comparator's +/-5 mV offset and unspecified input noise stop mattering. Overload: output clips at about 9 uA pp of photocurrent at 20k (36 uA at 5k), recovers in 3 ns (SBOS622D), and because the clip is symmetric about the AC baseline the comparator keeps slicing up to the TIA's own 60 uA saturation. A 60-80 MHz low-pass follows. Label: PROPOSED (clip timing shift and chain noise at 6 pF are G1 measurements; docs/research/post-amp-slicer.md).
5. **Baseline slicer (VCM_REF, SDET_P/SDET_N).** The comparator inputs are AC-coupled with matched returns (<= 1 k, TLV3605 bias current up to 5 uA) to a buffered 1.65 V reference; with DC-balanced 8b/10b the eye centre is that baseline, so no threshold tracking is needed. The ESP32 can shift the threshold by a few tens of mV through DAC_TH into a summing network (offset trim, eye walking). Label: PROPOSED.
6. **Range control (firmware trim).** Received intensity changes only as fast as the animal moves, so a tens-of-ms firmware loop is fast enough (Daniel, 2026-10-08). The ESP32 holds the APD near its optimum gain via DAC_HV and switches the OPA857 between 20k and 5k on TIA_CTRL, using an RSSI peak detector on the TIA output (crude, telemetry only) read by the ADS1115 and the FPGA's 8b/10b validity. At the sensitivity limit the Q-optimal M is 83 to 112 for the S12023-10 at the built 70 MHz noise bandwidth, capped at the datasheet's M = 100, so the weak end runs the APD at the top of its specified gain (link budget revision 2026-10-22, decision 31; the earlier "M 35-60" came from an inflated emitter figure). One fixed setting covers 10 to 12 dB optical unclipped (about 19 dB to OPA857 saturation); firmware adds 18 to 19 dB optical at the strong end only, since every knob lowers gain. Fade margin at the weak end therefore equals emitter margin. With the ESP32 unprogrammed the signal path runs at 20k but there is no APD bias, so the module is dark (decision 21); there is no resistor-set bias on Rev A. Label: PROPOSED.
7. **Signal presence.** The FPGA's decoder validity is the LOS judge; RSSI is advisory. There is no analog LOS and nothing to freeze (the integrator is gone). Label: PROPOSED.
8. **Removed with decision 12.** VCA821 VGA, LM27762 +/-3.7 V rails, 5V_A, the fast high/low capture coupon, VMID/VSPAN, the AGC integrator, TMUX1112 freeze, TLV3201 LOS, HOLD_N. History in decision-log entries 10 and 12 and in git.
10. **ECP5 FPGA (ULX3S first).** The FPGA does clock recovery, 8b/10b decode, framing and redundant-lane merge, because no clock is sent: a digital CDR is flexible, lets the rate change, and avoids a PLL on the module. Its watchdog uses an independent local clock and the CDR and framing are reacquired after release; invalid-code counts are not a BER measurement. ULX3S true pairs: gp/gn[8..13] (bank 7, VCCIO jumper-selectable 2.5/3.3 V), gp/gn[14..21] (banks 2/3, 3.3 V); banks 0/1 (gp/gn[0..7], [22..27]) have no differential inputs. ECP5 differential inputs are powered from VCCAUX, independent of VCCIO, and on-die DIFFRESISTOR=100 is available on the left/right banks (FPGA-TN-02032 v1.4 s.4.3, s.4.10, s.4.11.8; ULX3S v3.1.7 schematic p.2-3): VERIFIED, see docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md. No external termination or LVDS receiver IC at the FPGA input; on the module the 100 ohm termination sits at the fan-out buffer U50 input (decision 14). Polarity: light on pulls SDET_P below the baseline, so LVDS_P is low for a logic one, and the pairs are flipped at J50 (N to SSTXp, P to SSTXn on both pairs, 2026-10-09), so SSTXp is high for a one; the FPGA-end inversion is a gateware constant (docs/schematic/connector.md). ECP5 LVDS input: VCM 0.05-2.35 V and threshold +/-100 mV (FPGA-DS-02012-3.4 Table 3.13 p.58, VERIFIED 2026-10-12), so the TLV3605's 1.125-1.375 V common mode fits and its 250 mV minimum VOD leaves about 25 mV of threshold margin (G1 eye measurement decides). Gateware spec and status: firmware/ecp5/README.md (one ULX3S has 7 header pairs free of other loads, so 14 lanes on one board is UNRESOLVED there). Open: DIFFRESISTOR support in nextpnr. Label: PROPOSED.
11. **Module controller (ESP32-C6-MINI-1, decision 11).** Intended use includes continuous telemetry over CAN (RSSI, TIA gain, bias set point and HV_MON, temperature, identity, firmware version, HV state, CAN error counters) joined on the FPGA side with per-lane 8b/10b error counts for a per-module dashboard; design the CAN message set for that (Daniel, 2026-10-08). The module's single point of contact with the FPGA side over CAN (CAN_P/CAN_N on the SS RX pairs of the same USB-C cable, 3.3 V transceiver, RS 100 k for slow edges; master on the ULX3S side). Locally it drives an MCP4728 12-bit DAC (DAC_TH, DAC_HV; OUTA/OUTD are spare test pads) and reads an ADS1115 ADC (HV_MON, RSSI, VTH_MON) over I2C, and owns HV_DIS, SHDN_N, LE_HYS and TIA_CTRL on GPIO. It reports a unique module ID. It is not in the signal path: the signal path needs nothing from the ESP32 (TIA at 20k by pull-up, comparator on), but the APD bias is enabled only by the provisioned ESP32 driving HV_DIS low (or the J1 bench jumper, fitted only after a first firmware boot, decision 20), so an unprogrammed module is safe but dark (decision 21) (decision 12 and power.md). Radio unused. Own 3V3_D LDO rail. Native USB on the module's single 24-pin USB-C (decisions 13 and 14) for programming and standalone self-test; CC1_SENSE on IO4 and CC2_SENSE on IO5; CAN TX stays silent until bus traffic or the FPGA master is seen. Label: PROPOSED (parts and pin map in docs/research/module-controller.md).

## States

| State | Entry | Behavior | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| POWER-UP | Rails up; HV off (HV_DIS high) | ESP32 verifies or provisions the DAC EEPROM (decision 20), enables HV at the DAC full-scale floor (about 50 V, decision 19) and walks it to hv_min, TIA at 20k; comparator slices whatever arrives | ESP32 ready |
| SEARCH | No valid frames | Firmware steps M and 20k/5k from RSSI; FPGA searches for valid code | Valid frames |
| TRACK | Valid frames | Firmware trims M toward the Q(M) optimum slowly from RSSI and FPGA error counts | Loss of validity |
| LOS | FPGA timeout on an independent clock | Firmware returns to SEARCH scheduling; comparator state is "invalid" | Valid frames |

Dwell times and the RSSI thresholds are UNRESOLVED until G3. Provisional goal: reacquisition <= 20 ms (requirements.md item 3; not approved).

## Layout status (2026-10-12, PROPOSED)

Layout started 2026-10-09 and the schematic generators are frozen. Board: 6-layer JLCPCB JLC06161H-3313 (F.Cu, GND1, SIG_IN, PWR, GND2, B.Cu), ENIG, 75 x 50 mm placeholder outline (Daniel), four M2 plated mounting holes H1-H4 to GND, ordered on the high-precision via tier because of the J50 B-row holes (DFM test board accepted 2026-10-09, waiver rules in `receiver-module.kicad_dru`). All 221 footprints are placed by `tools/pcb_place.py` from `placement/placement-spec.json` (fast chain on the top centre line from the APD at the optical end to J50 at the cable end, HV island on the back edge, ESP32 and slow parts on the bottom; `docs/pcb/placement-plan.html`); routing has been complete since mid-October 2026 (0 unconnected pads, DRC baseline 48 items, all dispositioned in `docs/review/2026-10-22-rev-a-review/README.md`; all vias epoxy filled and capped, decision 30). Net classes HV (1.0 mm), HV_LADDER, DIFF90, USB90, CAN90, DIFF100 with 2D-solver geometries, PROPOSED until confirmed in the JLCPCB calculator (`hardware/pcb/receiver-module/README.md`, `docs/pcb/jlcpcb-fab-settings.md`). Layout-driven schematic hand edits so far: ESD channels split by position along J50, U50 output swap, LVDS polarity flip at J50, CAN_TX/CAN_RX swap to IO19/IO18, D11 dropped (decision-log entry 15); on 2026-10-22 the J50 shell was tied directly to GND (C181 and R170 removed, decision-log entry 16).
