# Decision log

Entries dated 2026-10-08 (user session) except where a date is given. Labels are PROPOSED unless noted.

## Decisions

1. **Analog-first.** AGC and slicing are analog; MCU/ADC hooks are optional. Rationale: user decision, lowest latency and independent of firmware.
2. **Single PCB, hierarchical sheets.** Rapid iteration instead of many coupons.
3. **ULX3S first FPGA target.** Available now. LVDS input on the 3.3 V banks VERIFIED (docs/research/ulx3s-lvds-input.md): ECP5 differential inputs run from VCCAUX and DIFFRESISTOR=100 exists on banks 7 and 2/3, so the TLV3605 pair goes straight to gp/gn[8..13] or [14..21] with no receiver IC and no external termination.
4. **Shielded housing and swappable filter.** 6.63 MHz QSCR field is in-band; one module serves 630 nm and 765 nm.
5. **Rails +/-3.7 V; APD_BIAS 100–220 V (superseded PD_BIAS >= 6.5 V on 2026-10-08).** See corrections 4 and 14 and decision 8.
6. **All new parts via lab-library PR.** No project-local symbols; library at ~/Projects/kicad-lab-library.
7. **Headstage co-designed with receiver; no backward compatibility requirement.** Emitter wavelength, radiant intensity, drive and modulation are co-design variables (NIR preferred); the link budget is joint. Miniscope Zero emitter facts are baseline only.
8. **Detector: silicon APD selected (2026-10-08).** The link budget (docs/calculations/link-budget.md) shows a bare FDS010 PIN into the OPA857 is 18–23 dB optical short of Q = 6 at 35 cm lateral with any headstage-power-compatible emitter, because photocurrent (1–3 nA) is far below TIA noise (8–26 nA). Étendue caps optics at ~2× for a ±45° field of view. An APD with M ≈ 50 plus a modestly brighter NIR emitter closes the link with margin. Daniel approved the APD path. Consequences: the 6.5–15 V MAX15032 bias block is replaced by a 150–200 V temperature-compensated APD bias supply; the FDS010 remains a drop-in fallback only if a compatible footprint is kept; part selection is tracked in docs/research/apd-selection.md. PROPOSED until a part and bias design are chosen. 2026-10-09: the FDS010 bench-fallback footprint D11 was dropped from Rev A (Daniel); the FDS010 remains a bench-only reference in requirements.md.

9. **APD bias supply: on-board LT8365 boost + charge-pump stage (2026-10-08).** Daniel prefers to handle as much as reasonable on our own PCB rather than a purchased HV module. Rev A therefore carries an LT8365-based 100–220 V generator (datasheet Rev. A and DC2838A demo manual Rev. 0 archived in docs/datasheets/), a two-stage RC cathode filter, series current limit, NTC temperature-tracking divider with a DAC-trim hook, and a footprint/pads for an external HV module as bench fallback. Design calculations in docs/calculations/apd-bias-design.md. PROPOSED until measured.

10. **Rev A schematic choices (2026-10-08, PROPOSED).** Captured in docs/schematic/rev-a-sheet-specs.md and the per-sheet as-built notes:
   - The analog LOS comparator (TLV3201 on VSPAN) is a status output only and does not freeze the AGC integrator. Reason: a weak eye at power-up would set LOS, freeze the integrator and leave the gain unable to rise (startup deadlock found during sheet population). With no signal the loop runs VG toward its upper bound, which is the SEARCH state in the architecture state table. The FPGA's HOLD_N (via a TMUX1112 switch in the integrator input path) is the only freeze; a DNP 0 ohm resistor keeps the LOS-freeze option for bench experiments. The VG upper bound versus the VCA821 0-2 V gain-control range is UNRESOLVED (BAS70 clamps footprinted, DNP).
   - Gain and threshold sources are 3-pin headers (VINT / pot / DAC and VMID / pot / DAC) because they change on the bench; the detector coupon and the OPA857 gain select use solder jumpers.
   - APD bias filter split: clamp + 27 k/10 nF on the Power sheet, 10 k/10 nF at the cathode on the Front End sheet; HV nets are prefixed HV_ and sit in the project net class HV (1.0 mm clearance). HV_MON (10 M/100 k, Vout/101) is the only HV observation point; no bare APD_BIAS pad.
   - No SAMD51 on Rev A: a 6-pin MCU header carries DAC_GAIN, DAC_TH, DAC_HV and HV_MON with 100 k pull-downs (polarity UNRESOLVED).
   - Standard KiCad power symbols (power:PWR_FLAG, power:GND) are allowed alongside the lab library, as in the lab's CaRacterize project.

11. **Module controller: ESP32-C6 on each receiver, CAN to the FPGA side, no BLE (2026-10-08, PROPOSED).** Daniel is moving away from the SAMD51 and wants each module to carry an ESP32 as its single point of contact with the FPGA side (module identification, future-proofing); the ESP32 then talks to everything slow on the module. Consequences: (a) on-board MCP4728 12-bit I2C DAC (DAC_GAIN, DAC_TH, DAC_HV trims) and ADS1115 16-bit I2C ADC (HV_MON, VSPAN, VG, VMID monitors) driven by the ESP32 over local I2C, replacing the external-MCU header; (b) CAN_P/CAN_N in the per-module cable next to the LVDS pair through a 3.3 V transceiver, cables joined star-fashion at the FPGA end, low bit rate so stubs are tolerable (physical-layer limits in docs/research/module-controller.md); the ULX3S's own ESP32 or an FPGA soft core is the master; (c) HOLD_N stays a direct FPGA wire so the AGC freeze does not depend on firmware; (d) SHDN_N, LE_HYS, HV_EN and LOS/PGOOD readback move to ESP32 GPIO; (e) a separate 3V3_D LDO rail for the ESP32 keeps its current steps off the analog 3V3; (f) the radio is unpopulated in use, so the shielded housing needs no RF window; BLE was considered and rejected (shielding, TX noise next to the TIA, and a wired bus already exists). Analog-first is unchanged: the ESP32 is not in the signal path and the module works with the ESP32 unprogrammed (manual trimmers, HOLD_N wire).

12. **Fixed-gain post-amplifier and baseline slicer replace the VCA821 VGA, analog AGC loop and peak detectors (2026-10-08, PROPOSED, approved by Daniel).** Chain: OPA857 TIA -> AC coupling -> OPA855 fixed gain x7 -> 60-80 MHz low-pass -> AC coupling with matched <= 1 k returns to a 1.65 V reference -> TLV3605 with 2-4 mV external hysteresis -> LVDS. Slow range control is done by the ESP32 (APD bias via DAC_HV, OPA857 20k/5k select on a GPIO) using an RSSI peak detector read by the ADS1115 and the FPGA's 8b/10b validity. Basis (docs/research/post-amp-slicer.md): with 8b/10b the eye centre is the AC-coupled baseline, so no threshold tracking is needed; the OPA855 recovers from rail clipping in 3 ns (UI 20 ns) and clipping is symmetric about the baseline, so the comparator keeps slicing up to the TIA's own 60 uA saturation; one fixed setting covers ~39 dB of signal range and firmware adds ~32 dB; the comparator sees 105-175 mVpp at the operating point so its +/-5 mV offset and unspecified input noise stop mattering. Daniel's note: received intensity changes only as fast as the animal moves, slow relative to a firmware trim loop, so an analog AGC buys nothing. Removed: VCA821, LM27762 +/-3.7 V rails, 5V_A, the detector coupon (OPA356 x2, BAS70 x2, OPA2320), VMID/VSPAN/integrator/TMUX1112/TLV3201 and HOLD_N (cable pin becomes AUX2). Kept: analog slicing, module runs with the ESP32 unprogrammed at a fixed M and 20k. Standing corrections 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 are superseded (kept below for history); correction 11 still applies. Hard rule 1 is read as "slicing stays analog; range control is a firmware trim".

13. **USB standalone power and programming (2026-10-09, PROPOSED until G1 measurements, approved by Daniel; the TPS2121 mux, PWR_SRC, VBUS_SENSE, J60 and J50 parts are superseded by decision 14).**
   - What: a TI TPS2121 priority power mux (U5) merges the system-cable 5 V (+5V_CABLE, IN1, priority) and USB-C VBUS (IN2, fallback) into the internal `+5V` rail. RESET (EN) and BOOT (IO9) tactile switches, 22 ohm series resistors on D+/D- (R193/R194, with DNP capacitor footprints), a VBUS_SENSE divider on IO4 and the mux status PWR_SRC on IO5. Detail: docs/schematic/rev-a-sheet-specs.md revision 3; facts and citations: docs/research/usb-standalone-power.md.
   - Why: Daniel's requirement (2026-10-09): "anyone should be able to plug a module into a random laptop over USB, program it and verify it, with no FPGA attached". The native USB Serial/JTAG supports ESPHome-style browser flashing and host-driven reset/download (TRM v1.2 p.1030), and the self-test needs power without the cable. When both sources are plugged in the TPS2121 powers the module from the cable, and its reverse-current blocking keeps either source from being back-driven (SLVSEA3F p.7, p.15; PROPOSED, to be measured).
   - Alternatives rejected: two LM66100 ideal diodes (no native priority, CE compares against VIN so the higher rail wins and the band between is undefined; 6 V abs max can be exceeded by a hot-plug ring; two new library parts). Schottky OR-ing (no priority, the higher rail wins, costs 0.3-0.45 V at the load, no soft start or current limit; the Vf figure is typical Schottky behaviour, not read from a datasheet).
   - Consequences: new lab-library category `AharoniLab_Switch` (symbol PTS810, footprint SW_SPST_PTS810, 3D model) by library PR; `+5V` is renamed `+5V_CABLE` on the cable side (Connector -> Power) and `+5V` stays the merged internal rail; new hierarchical nets VBUS (Controller -> Power) and PWR_SRC (Power -> Controller). Worst-case input about 0.19 A (0.24 A with margin) against the 0.5 A USB default: the radio must stay off. HV rules unchanged: HV_DIS default off, HV starts only when the ESP32 drives HV_DIS low, and in USB-alone mode firmware starts HV from the low-bias end of DAC_HV in steps because a faulted HV start can trip a USB port. UNRESOLVED items listed in docs/research/usb-standalone-power.md section 7 (esptool auto-reset through 22R and the USBLC6, mux settling and inrush, cable-rail chatter, USB spec limits, ground offset). Gate: G1 measurements (docs/bringup/gates.md).

14. **Single 24-pin USB-C connector per module (decision 14, 2026-10-09, PROPOSED until G1 and G4 measurements, approved by Daniel).**
   - What: one 24-pin full-featured USB-C receptacle (Wuerth 632723300011, horizontal) per module replaces the 16-pin USB-C J60 and the 12-pin system header J50. It carries VBUS/GND (5 V), D+/D- (ESP32-C6 USB Serial/JTAG, Dp1 tied to Dp2 and Dn1 to Dn2), the module LVDS on both SS TX pairs through an SN65LVDS104PW fan-out buffer (U50), CAN_P/CAN_N bused on both SS RX pairs [nets renamed CAN_P/CAN_N and USB_DN 2026-10-16 for KiCad diff-pair routing; no electrical change], and SBU1/SBU2 tied as AUX. Mated through a passive full-featured C-to-C cable. A TI TPS259631DDAR eFuse (U5) replaces the TPS2121 and is the only 5 V input stage. CC1/CC2 are read on IO4/IO5. Detail: docs/schematic/rev-a-sheet-specs.md revision 4; adopted draft and facts: docs/research/decision-14-draft-usbc-single-connector.md (with usbc-connector-and-cable.md, usbc-power-path.md, lvds-fanout-and-foreign-host.md).
   - Why (Daniel, 2026-10-09): one connector and one standard cable per module; plug orientation must not cost ECP5 LVDS pairs (with the pairs duplicated and CAN bused, all four flip combinations land correctly on the far end, TC R2.5 Table 3-10 p.83; PROPOSED analysis); USB stays a line to a computer. System picture: CAN + LVDS between the FPGA and the modules, USB only toward a computer (one hub on the FPGA-end breakout board; the ULX3S cannot be a USB host).
   - Daniel's approvals (2026-10-09): (1) horizontal receptacle, Wuerth 632723300011; (2) TPS259631DDAR eFuse replaces the TPS2121 (OV cut-off 5.76-6.20 V, UVLO rising 4.06-4.32 V, ILIM 0.76 A typ, calculated, not simulated); (3) J51 kept as a scope header on the fan-out buffer's spare output 3; (4) cables: <= 2 m certified passive full-featured (USB 3.2 Gen 1, 3 A, e-marked); 3 m only after a bench check (SS-wire continuity then scope) of a specific passive cable.
   - Supersedes in decision 13: the TPS2121 two-source mux, `+5V_CABLE`, PWR_SRC, the VBUS_SENSE divider (R215/R216/C205), the 16-pin J60 and the 12-pin J50, AUX2. Keeps decision 13's requirement (a module can be plugged into a random laptop, programmed and verified with no FPGA) and its RESET/BOOT switches, 22 ohm D+/D- resistors and HV discipline (HV_DIS default off; stepped start in USB-alone mode). R199 (SN65HVD230 RS) goes 10 k -> 100 k for slower CAN edges on 3 m stubs.
   - Standing corrections: the list below is unchanged.
   - New lab-library parts (library PRs): Wuerth 632723300011 symbol and footprint, SN65LVDS104PW symbol, TPS259631DDAR symbol and a PowerPAD SOIC-8 footprint, TPD6E05U06RVZ symbol and USON-14 footprint, in addition to decision 13's AharoniLab_Switch.
   - Host link (open, a separate decision): how the ULX3S/FPGA end talks to a computer is NOT decided here (draft open item 8). Daniel's preference to evaluate first: the Cypress FX3 (CYUSB3014 family, GPIF II) which the lab knows from the Miniscope DAQ. Other options are recorded in the draft (USB 2.0 or USB 3.0 FIFO bridge, Ethernet PHY, single-board computer).
   - Open items: draft section 6 (items 1-16): 3 m cable existence; laptop foreign-host bench test with two hosts; eFuse thresholds/inrush; CC ADC classification margin; footprint checks; CAN with 14 stubs; CAN-to-LVDS crosstalk; host link; breakout board design. All UNRESOLVED until measured (G1, G4 in docs/bringup/gates.md).

## Standing corrections (from the prior design review)

Corrections 1, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9 refer to the VCA821/AGC chain and are superseded by decision 12 (kept for history).

1. OPA857 OUT/OUTN are AC-coupled into the VCA821, never DC-wired. Rationale: OUTN is about 1.83 V; VCA821 +IN common-mode limit is +1.6 V.
2. Photodiode/APD cathode to positive bias (through the RC filter and current limit), anode to OPA857 IN; reverse bias is about cathode voltage minus 1.83 V. Rationale: sets polarity and real bias. Applies unchanged to the S12023-10 APD.
3. FDS010 capacitance is 6 pF typ at 10 V reverse bias. VERIFIED 2026-10-08 against Thorlabs 0636-S01 Rev I (March 16, 2017), docs/datasheets/fds010.pdf p.1; the older Rev E 2 pF figure is superseded. Pinout verified from the Rev I drawing (p.3): pin 1 anode, pin 2 case, pin 3 cathode.
4. +/-3.5 V is the VCA821 minimum; nominal rails +/-3.7 V (tolerance analysis pending). Rationale: margin above the minimum.
5. VCA821 RF is compensation; use datasheet networks; RG current limit about +/-2.6 mA can overload the input even at low gain. Rationale: stability and input overload.
6. Unity buffer plus diode is not a precision peak detector; the fast high/low detector is the main unresolved block, built as a disconnectable, swappable sub-circuit. Rationale: accuracy and risk isolation.
7. VSPAN subtractor: R41 VLOW->IN-, R42 OUT->IN-, R43 VHIGH->IN+, R44 IN+->GND, all 10k 0.1% matched. Rationale: ratio accuracy sets span error.
8. VMID: two matched 10k into a high-impedance buffer; threshold trim via a source selector or proper summing stage, never a resistor into a buffer output. Rationale: a resistor into a buffer output does not sum.
9. AGC is a true integrator (C in feedback), 100k/47 nF giving 4.7 ms; anti-windup acts on integrator state; freeze is not reset. Rationale: zero steady-state error and defined recovery.
10. Post-AGC span alone cannot prove signal presence; gain bounds, dwell and FPGA digital validity decide. Rationale: AGC amplifies noise to target.
11. TLV3605 SHDN is active-low; shutdown is not a guaranteed zero; LE/HYS low latches the last state; both constant LVDS states are "invalid" after an FPGA timeout on an independent clock. Rationale: avoid mistaking a stuck output for data.
12. FPGA watchdog runs on an independent local clock; reacquire CDR and framing after release; invalid-code counts are not a BER measurement. Rationale: watchdog must survive loss of recovered clock; counts miss errors.
13. SAMD51 DACs are 12-bit; OPA320 is single, OPA2320 dual. Rationale: correct resolution and part selection.
14. LM27762 positive/negative references differ (1.2 V vs 1.22 V), so rails use different divider ratios. Rationale: regulator limits. (The MAX15032 VIN+1 V bound, bias rail >= 6.5 V, applied to the PIN design and is retired with decision 8; the APD bias supply is selected in docs/research/apd-bias-supply.md.)
